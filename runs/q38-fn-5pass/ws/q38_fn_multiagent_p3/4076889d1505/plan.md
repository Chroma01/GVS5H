Factor each N into prime powers. For each odd prime power p^e, use local modulus p^{e+1} and residue 1+p, whose order is exactly p^e; for powers of two use small dedicated moduli/residues. Combine all local congruences by CRT to obtain one A modulo M, and output A,M, with N=1 handled as (1,1). The product of local moduli is at most N^2 <= 10^18, and the global order is the lcm of the local prime-power orders, hence N.
