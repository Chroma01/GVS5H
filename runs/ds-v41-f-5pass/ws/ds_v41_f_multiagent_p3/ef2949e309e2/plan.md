We need count index-based subsequences of length 5 whose selected middle position is a unique mode, modulo 1e9+7. The natural approach is to enumerate the middle selected index and count valid ways to choose two positions to its left and two to its right. Coordinate-compress values so frequency conditions can be checked efficiently. For each middle value x, the selected five positions are valid exactly when the total count of x among the five exceeds the count of every other value. Use combinatorial counting over left/right frequency buckets, avoiding explicit enumeration of all 5-tuples. Aim for an O(n^2) or O(n^2 log n) solution suitable for n <= 1000.
