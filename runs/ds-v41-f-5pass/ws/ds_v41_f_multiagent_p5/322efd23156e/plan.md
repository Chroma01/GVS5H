The problem can be solved by binary searching the answer threshold t, since feasibility is monotone: if a minimum intake of t is achievable, any smaller value is also achievable. For each vitamin separately, precompute the maximum amount obtainable for every calorie limit up to X using a 0/1 knapsack DP over foods of that vitamin. Then for a candidate t, compute the minimum calories needed for each vitamin to reach at least t, and check whether their sum is at most X. The vitamins are independent because each food belongs to exactly one vitamin, so the minimum total calorie cost is the sum of the three individual minimum costs. The maximum feasible t is the answer.
