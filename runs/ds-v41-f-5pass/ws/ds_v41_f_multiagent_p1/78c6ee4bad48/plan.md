The operation only changes three consecutive gaps between sorted coordinates; it swaps the outer two gaps while keeping the middle gap. Therefore gaps in odd positions and even positions each form independent multisets that can be arbitrarily permuted. The total coordinate sum is fixed by the first coordinate plus a weighted sum of gaps, where the weight for gap j is N-j. We need choose permutations within each parity class to minimize this weighted sum, then output the resulting minimum. This becomes an independent rearrangement-inequality problem for odd-indexed and even-indexed gaps.
