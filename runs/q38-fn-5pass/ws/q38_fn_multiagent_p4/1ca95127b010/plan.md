Operation A is the inverse of Operation B, and it moves a block of Y ones left by X positions (equivalently, moves X zeros right by Y positions). Therefore, the relative order of all 1s and all 0s is preserved, and the 0-based index of each 1 modulo X and each 0 modulo Y are invariant. These ordered residue sequences are also complete: repeatedly applying Operation A yields a unique canonical form determined only by them, so equality of the sequences is necessary and sufficient. Thus, the decision procedure is to compare the number of 1s, the sequence of 1-positions modulo X, and the sequence of 0-positions modulo Y for S and T. This gives an O(N) time solution.
