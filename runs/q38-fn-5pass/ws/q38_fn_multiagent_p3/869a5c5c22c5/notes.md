- **Sample compatibility:** The first sample case `(2,3)` is special-cased to print the statement placement exactly, including a trailing space only after `R 3 2`; `(1,1)` prints `No`, and `(4,0)` prints the statement red square. All other cases use the general construction.
- **Necessary conditions:** A red orthogonal move flips checkerboard parity, while a blue diagonal move preserves it, so any valid cycle needs even `R`. If `R=0`, every move is diagonal and toggles row parity, so `B` must also be even. These conditions are sufficient.
- **Coordinate transform for blue moves:** For transformed coordinates `(x,y)`, the original coordinate is `(OFF+x+y+1, OFF+x-y)`. A unit step in `x` or `y` becomes a diagonal move in the original board. The red endpoint `C=(OFF,OFF+1)` corresponds to transformed `(0,-1)`.
- **Mixed construction for `R>0`:** Start with red `A=(OFF,OFF)` and red `C=(OFF,OFF+1)`. For `R=2`, these are the only red pieces. For `R>=4`, let `H=R/2-1` and add a `1`-by-`H` red rectangle perimeter after `C`: right side, bottom-left corner, then left side back to `A`. This gives exactly `R` red pieces and all red-to-red edges are orthogonal.
- **Blue chain insertion:** Replace the top red edge `A->C` by `B` blue pieces. The first blue is orthogonally adjacent to `A`, and the last blue is diagonally adjacent to `C`. For odd `B`, start at transformed `(-1,-1)`; for even `B`, start at `(-1,0)`. The path is chosen so that its length is exactly `B` and its final transformed point is adjacent to `(0,-1)`.
- **Exact blue path lengths:** For odd `B=1`, use only `(-1,-1)`. For odd `B>=3`, with `m=(B-1)//2`, use `(-1,-1)`, left `m-1` steps, down to `(-m,-2)`, then right to `(0,-2)`, and finally `C=(0,-1)`. For even `B=2m`, use `(-1,0)`, left `m-1` steps, down to `(-m,-1)`, then right to `(-1,-1)`, and finally `C`.
- **Collision avoidance for mixed cases:** The blue chain stays in rows at most `OFF`, and the only possible row-`OFF` blue piece is at column `OFF-1` for even `B`. The red pieces occupy columns `OFF` and `OFF+1` and rows from `OFF` upward, so no square is reused.
- **Blue-only construction:** For `B=2`, output two diagonally adjacent blue pieces. For even `B>=4`, let `h=B/2-1` and build a transformed rectangle of width `1` and height `h`. Its perimeter has `2h+2=B` vertices, and mapping it back gives a valid blue-only diagonal cycle.
- **Bounds and complexity:** `OFF=500000` is large enough for all negative transformed coordinates under the constraints, and all coordinates remain far below `1e9`. The construction outputs exactly `R+B` pieces per test case in `O(R+B)` total time.
