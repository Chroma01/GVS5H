The cost is separable and convex, and the marginal cost of buying one more unit of a product currently at x units is (2x+1)P_i. Therefore, for a fixed total number of units T, the minimum cost is obtained by taking the T cheapest marginal costs across all products, which can be characterized by a Lagrange threshold. We can binary search the maximum feasible T, using an efficient feasibility check that counts how many marginal costs are at most a threshold and sums their costs. The inner check must run in roughly O(N) or O(N log A) per feasibility call for N up to 2e5. Finally, output the largest T whose minimum cost does not exceed M.
