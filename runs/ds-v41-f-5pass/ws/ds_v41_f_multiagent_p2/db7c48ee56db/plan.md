The key observation is that the constraint C(N,K) ≤ 10^6 forces S = min(K, N-K) to be at most 11, because for S ≥ 12, even C(2S, S) exceeds 10^6. Therefore we only need to enumerate subsets of size S, which is at most 1e6 combinations. If K ≤ N-K, we enumerate all K-combinations directly and take the maximum XOR. If K > N-K, we enumerate all (N-K)-combinations of excluded elements and combine their XOR with the total XOR of all elements to get the chosen set's XOR. The case K = N (S = 0) is handled separately by simply outputting the total XOR.
