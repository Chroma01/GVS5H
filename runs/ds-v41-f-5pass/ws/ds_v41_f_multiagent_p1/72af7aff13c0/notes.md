- **Answer formula:** T (path-product sum) is multilinear in A(c). Changing A(c) old->x: T += (x-old)*pre(c)*suf(c), where pre(c)=path-sum (1,1)->c excluding c, suf(c)=path-sum c->(H,W) excluding c. pre=1 at (1,1), suf=1 at (H,W).
- **DP:** F(c)=A(c)*(F(up)+F(left)) (forward), G(c)=A(c)*(G(down)+G(right)) (backward). pre(c)=F(up)+F(left); suf(c)=G(down)+G(right); T initialized as F(H,W) then maintained incrementally.
- **Why incremental works:** maintain F correct for all diagonals <= current d, G correct for all diagonals >= d. A change at diagonal d only updates Fpad[d][i] and Gpad[d][i] locally; pre from F[d-1] (d-1<=d, valid) and suf from G[d+1] (d+1>=d, valid). Forward step recomputes F of the new diagonal from the previously-correct F. Backward step recomputes G from valid G. Answer T never needs a full F(H,W) refresh.
- **Diagonal layout:** anti-diagonal d=h+w stored as array indexed by row with one zero pad each end (index = row-lo[d]+1). lo[d]=max(1,d-W), hi[d]=min(H,d-1), Nd[d]=hi-lo+1. lo increases by 0 or 1 per step, so slice offsets s are 0/-1 and starts s+1,s+2 in {0,1,2}; lengths equal Nd[d] with no clipping.
- **Unified kernel offsets:** forward s=lo[d]-lo[d-1]-1; backward s=lo[d]-lo[d+1]. Pure-python uses sp=lo[d]-lo[d-1] (forward) and sp=lo[d]-lo[d+1]+1 (backward).
