The cost increase for the j-th unit of product i is `(2j - 1) * P_i`, so the optimal strategy is to buy units in increasing order of these marginal costs. For a candidate marginal cost `X`, the number of units of product i whose marginal cost is at most `X` is `((X // P_i) + 1) // 2`, and their total cost is `P_i * t^2`. Binary search the largest `X` such that the total cost of all marginal units with cost at most `X` is at most `M`. At that point, all those units are affordable, and the next cheapest marginal cost is `X + 1`; spend the remaining budget on `remaining // (X + 1)` additional units. This yields an `O(N log 2e9)` solution.
