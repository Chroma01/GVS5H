Convert the sorted coordinates into adjacent gaps. The operation on four consecutive pieces reverses the three corresponding gaps, which is equivalent to swapping the first and third gaps, i.e. two gaps whose 1-based indices have the same parity. Therefore the gaps on odd indices and the gaps on even indices can each be permuted arbitrarily, but gaps never change parity. The total coordinate sum equals `N * X_1` plus a fixed weighted sum of gaps, with weight `N - j` for the `j`-th gap, and `X_1` is invariant. By the rearrangement inequality, minimize each parity class independently by pairing larger gaps with smaller weights, i.e. larger gap indices.
