The key reduction is that for any candidate answer d ≤ side, two boundary points have Manhattan distance < d iff their clockwise perimeter distance along the square is < d. Hence the problem becomes selecting k points on a circle of circumference 4*side with pairwise circular distance at least d, and the answer is known to be ≤ side for k ≥ 4. Binary search on d in [0, side]; for each d, sort the perimeter coordinates and check feasibility via a greedy circular packing test. For a fixed starting point, greedily take the next point at least d ahead; if k points are taken and the wrap-around gap is also ≥ d, d is feasible. Trying all starting points covers the circular case.
