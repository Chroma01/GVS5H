Extract the positions of all `1`s in the string. If there are `k` ones, their final contiguous block must occupy positions `x, x+1, ..., x+k-1` for some integer `x`. Minimizing adjacent swaps is equivalent to minimizing the sum of absolute distances between the original one-positions and these target positions. Transform each one-position `p_i` to `p_i - i`, then choose `x` as the median of these transformed values to minimize the total cost. Compute the sum of absolute deviations from the median as the answer.
