The shortest extension is obtained by keeping S and appending the reverse of the prefix that remains after the longest palindromic suffix of S. Equivalently, find the maximum k such that S[n-k:] is a palindrome, then append reverse(S[:n-k]). Compute k efficiently in O(n) or reliable O(n log n), since n can be 500000. Finally construct the answer and verify it is a palindrome with prefix S. Pay attention to memory usage and fast input/output.
