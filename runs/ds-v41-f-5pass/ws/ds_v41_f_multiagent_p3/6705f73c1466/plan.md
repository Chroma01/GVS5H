Extract the positions of all '1' characters in the original string. Transform each position by subtracting its ordinal index among the 1s, reducing the problem to choosing one integer center for these transformed values. The minimum adjacent swaps equals the minimum sum of absolute deviations from that center, attained at a median. Compute this sum in linear time using the median of the transformed values. Handle the single-1 edge case naturally, since the cost will be zero.
