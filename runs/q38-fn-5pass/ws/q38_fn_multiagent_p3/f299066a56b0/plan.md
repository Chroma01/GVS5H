Because the mochi sizes are already sorted, the problem becomes finding the maximum number of disjoint pairs where the smaller element is at most half of the larger element. A feasible way to make K pairs is to pair the K smallest mochi with the K largest mochi, checking that each small mochi can be placed on its corresponding large mochi. We can binary search K from 0 to N/2 and test feasibility in linear time, or use a greedy two-pointer scan to count pairs directly. The final answer is the largest feasible K.
