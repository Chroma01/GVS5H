Factor each N into prime powers. For every odd prime power p^e in N, use a component modulus p^{e+1} and residue 1+p, whose multiplicative order is exactly p^e. For the 2-part, use modulus 4 with residue 3 when N is divisible by 2 but not 4, and modulus 2^{e+2} with residue 5 when 2^e divides N with e >= 2. Combine all pairwise coprime component moduli by CRT to obtain A and M; the order of A modulo M is the lcm of the component orders, hence N. Handle N=1 separately with (1,1), and the constructed M is at most N^2 <= 10^18.
