Treat each permutation's concatenation as a sum over elements x of x shifted by the total digit length of elements placed after x. For a fixed x, summing over all permutations reduces to summing over subsets S of the other elements that appear after x, with weight |S|!(N-1-|S|)!10^{sum of digit lengths in S}. Since only digit lengths 1 through 6 matter, all x with the same length have the same subset-sum factor; multiply that factor by the sum of their values. Compute the subset-sum factor for each possible excluded length by forming the polynomial ∏(1+10^d t)^{c_d'} whose coefficient of t^k is the weighted sum over subsets of size k, then combine coefficients with k!(N-1-k)!. Use a logarithmic-derivative recurrence to obtain all coefficients efficiently, and accumulate the answer modulo 998244353.
