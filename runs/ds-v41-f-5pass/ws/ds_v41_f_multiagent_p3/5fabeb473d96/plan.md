The total number of trees is (N-1)!, so each answer is (N-1)! times the sum over edges i of A_i times the probability that exactly one of u,v lies in the subtree of i. In the random recursive tree model, a vertex x>i is in the subtree of i with probability 1/i, and two distinct vertices x,y>i are both in that subtree with probability 2/(i(i+1)). These facts give a simple piecewise contribution for each edge depending on whether i is below u, equal to u, between u and v, or equal to v. Therefore all queries reduce to prefix sums of A_i/i and A_i*2(i-1)/(i(i+1)), plus modular inverses and a factorial.
