Reduce f(x,y) to the bottleneck edge on a path in a minimum spanning tree: f(x,y) equals the maximum edge weight on the MST path. Build a Kruskal reconstruction tree, where f(x,y) becomes the weight of the LCA of leaves x and y. Then minimize the sum of LCA weights over a perfect pairing between the multisets A and B using a bottom-up tree DP over the reconstruction tree. The DP pairs as many A/B elements as possible inside lower subtrees and pays the current node weight for unavoidable cross-subtree pairs. Implement this in O((N+M) log N) and validate carefully.
