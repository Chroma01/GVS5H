Work over the field modulo prime p. For odd p, expand (A + sum x_e E_e)^p as a sum of length-p words in A and the matrix units E_e for zero positions; summing x_e over F_p^* kills every word except those with no variables or with exactly one variable appearing p-1 times. Both surviving cases have total coefficient (-1)^K, where K is the number of zeros, so the answer is (-1)^K times A^p plus the aggregate contribution of all single-variable words. For a diagonal zero E_ii, the single-variable contribution is A E_ii + E_ii A - 2 A_ii E_ii, i.e. add column i and row i of A except the diagonal; for an off-diagonal zero E_ij it is nonzero only when p=3, where it adds A_ji to position (i,j). For p=2 there is only one replacement matrix, so compute its square directly.
