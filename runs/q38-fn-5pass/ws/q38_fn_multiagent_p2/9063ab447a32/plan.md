Model each additional unit as a marginal cost: the j-th unit of product i costs (2j-1)P_i. The optimal strategy is to buy the cheapest marginal units, so binary search the largest marginal-cost threshold C such that buying all units with marginal cost at most C costs no more than M. For a threshold C, product i contributes x_i = (C // P_i + 1) // 2 units and cost P_i * x_i^2; sum these costs, capping early when the total exceeds M. After finding the maximum feasible C, compute the exact number of units and cost for that threshold, then use the remaining budget to buy as many units as possible whose next marginal cost is C+1.
