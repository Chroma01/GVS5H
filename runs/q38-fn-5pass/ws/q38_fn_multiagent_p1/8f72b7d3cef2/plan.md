Model the process as expanding a contiguous interval; call an interval stable if its sum is not greater than either adjacent outside slime, treating missing neighbors as infinite. The answer for each starting slime is the minimal stable interval containing it. Stable intervals are laminar; singleton stable intervals are immediate answers, and every non-singleton stable interval has outside boundaries strictly larger than all inside elements, so it corresponds exactly to a subtree of a max Cartesian tree. Build such a tree with a deterministic tie-break, mark subtrees whose sum is at most their parent value, and for each index take the deepest marked stable ancestor unless its singleton is stable.
