Model the removal process as building a non-crossing matching of sequence positions, with one unpaired element allowed when N is odd; each matched pair contributes the absolute difference. Convert this to assigning one endpoint of every pair a plus sign and the other a minus sign, so the total is a signed sum with equal numbers of plus and minus signs. Show that the maximum signed sum is achieved by putting plus signs on the largest floor(N/2) values and minus signs on the smallest floor(N/2) values, ignoring the median for odd N. Prove that any such balanced sign assignment can be realized by a valid non-crossing matching, so the order of A does not affect the optimum. The resulting algorithm is to sort A and compute the difference of the upper and lower halves, with special handling only for O(N log N) sorting and 64-bit sums.
