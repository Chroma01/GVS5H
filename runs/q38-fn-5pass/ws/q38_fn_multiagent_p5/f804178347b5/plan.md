Model the repeated majority operation as a complete ternary tree whose leaves are the characters of A and whose root is A'_1. For each node, compute the minimum number of leaf changes needed to force that node’s value to 0 and to force it to 1. For a leaf, these costs are 0 and 1 depending on its original bit; for an internal node with three children, forcing value v requires at least two children to be v, so combine child costs by choosing the two cheapest children to set to v and the cheapest option for the remaining child. Compute these costs bottom-up from the leaves to the root, then output the cost to force the root to the opposite of its current value.
