Use prefix sums of `nums` and `cost` to express each subarray cost in terms of boundary positions. Rewrite the total partition cost as a constant base cost plus a sum of edge weights between consecutive cut boundaries, eliminating the need to track the number of subarrays explicitly. Then solve a shortest-path DP on the DAG of boundary indices, where `dp[j]` is the minimum extra cost to reach boundary `j` as the last cut or the end. The final answer is the base cost plus `dp[n]`. This gives an `O(n^2)` time, `O(n)` memory solution that fits `n <= 1000`.
