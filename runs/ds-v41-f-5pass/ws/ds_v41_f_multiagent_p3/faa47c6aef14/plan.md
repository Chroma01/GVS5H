Model each color's movement as traversing a permutation cycle; first check all red/blue balls lie in the P/Q cycle containing X, otherwise impossible. For each color, find the farthest initial ball from X along its cycle; this determines a required operation chain from that node toward the predecessor of X. The answer is the length of the shortest common supersequence of the red and blue chains. Since nodes are unique within each chain, this equals |red| + |blue| - LCS(red, blue), and LCS can be computed as an LIS after mapping blue-chain nodes to positions in the red chain.
