For each index as the middle element, choose two elements from the left and two from the right. Let x be the middle value and k be how many of the four chosen elements equal x; the subsequence is valid iff k >= 2, or k == 1 and the three non-x chosen elements are all distinct. Maintain prefix/suffix frequency counts while sweeping the middle index, and use counts of values to compute pair categories (0, 1, or 2 copies of x) in O(1) per side. For the k == 1 cases, sum over the non-x value y paired with x on one side and multiply by the number of distinct-value pairs on the other side that exclude x and y. Combine these counts modulo 10^9 + 7 in O(n * U), where U is the number of distinct values.
