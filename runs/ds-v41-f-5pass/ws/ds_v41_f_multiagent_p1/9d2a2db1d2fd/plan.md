The problem reduces to finding, for each query, the highest floor level at which the two buildings remain connected through a path of buildings with sufficient height. This is a bottleneck connectivity problem on a grid graph where edges connect adjacent buildings with weight equal to the minimum of their floor counts. Build a maximum spanning forest (or Kruskal reconstruction tree) so that the maximum floor level connecting any two buildings is given by the minimum edge weight on the tree path. Preprocess this tree for fast LCA queries to retrieve that bottleneck value. For each query, combine the bottleneck with the minimum of the two starting floors to compute the minimal stair cost. Finally, output all answers.
