For a fixed path, any sequence of floor changes has stair cost at least |Y-k|+|Z-k| where k is the lowest floor used, and that k is feasible as a single walkway level, so the optimal cost is max(|Y-Z|, Y+Z-2B). Compute all-pairs bottleneck values by building a maximum spanning structure on the grid with edge weight min(F_u,F_v); a Kruskal reconstruction tree makes B the weight of the LCA of the two cell nodes.
