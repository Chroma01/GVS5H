Model the repeated majority operation as a ternary tree: leaves are characters of A, and each internal node is the majority of its three children. Compute DP for every subtree node returning the minimum flips needed to force that node to 0 or 1. Leaves have base costs from their original bit; internal nodes combine three child DP pairs by checking all 8 assignments whose majority equals the target. Since 3^N ≤ 3^13, an O(3^N) bottom-up DP is feasible. Finally, output the DP cost for the root to become the opposite of its original value.
