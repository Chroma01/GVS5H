Process the insertions in reverse: when only numbers 1..i are present, number i was inserted at position P_i. Thus the final position of i is the P_i-th still-unassigned slot from the left among all N final positions. Assign numbers from N down to 1 into such slots. Use a Fenwick tree or segment tree to find the k-th empty slot and mark it occupied efficiently. Finally, read out the values in position order. This gives O(N log N) time and O(N) memory, suitable for N up to 5×10^5.
