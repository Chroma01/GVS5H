Normalize each conflicting pair to `(a, b)` with `a < b`. For a fixed right endpoint `r`, a subarray ending at `r` is valid iff its left endpoint is greater than the maximum `a` among all remaining pairs with `b <= r`. Thus the total valid subarrays for a set of pairs is the sum over `r` of `r - L(r)`, where `L(r)` is that maximum. Removing a pair only improves the count at endpoints where it is the unique active maximum; then `L(r)` drops to the second-largest active `a`. Scan `r = 1..n`, add pairs whose `b == r`, maintain the largest and second-largest active `a` values plus the count/id of the largest, and accumulate both the base total and the per-pair gain.
