Count the frequencies of the 26 lowercase letters. A final good string is fully described by a target frequency f and, for each letter, a final count of either 0 or f; it is sufficient to try f from 1 to len(s). For a fixed f, the baseline cost is the sum of abs(freq[i] - chosen_i), representing deletions and insertions. The only way a forward change can improve this baseline is by moving one surplus character to the next letter's deficit, saving one operation; moving two or more steps is no better than deleting and inserting, and z cannot wrap to a. Use a constant-size DP over the alphabet with state chosen_i in {0, f}, subtracting adjacent surplus-to-deficit savings, and take the minimum over all f.
