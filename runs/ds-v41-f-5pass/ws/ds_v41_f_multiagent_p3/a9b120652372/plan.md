The operation moves all pieces one step toward a chosen pivot, so pairwise distances between pieces never increase and each piece moves at most one step per operation. Thus the final occupied set B must be obtainable by shifting and merging initial pieces without increasing any gap between consecutive occupied squares. Reachability reduces to checking whether the sequence of gaps between consecutive 1s in B can be matched, in order, to a subsequence of gaps between consecutive 1s in A, each A-gap being at least the corresponding B-gap. If feasible, the minimum number of operations is the maximum distance the leftmost and rightmost pieces must travel, i.e., max(|firstB-firstA|, |lastB-lastA|). This yields an O(N) per test case algorithm using two pointers over the extracted 1-positions.
