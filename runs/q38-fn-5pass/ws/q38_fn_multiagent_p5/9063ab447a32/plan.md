Use binary search on the maximum total number of units, since the feasibility of buying at least X units is monotonic. For a fixed X, minimize the cost by choosing the X cheapest marginal costs, where the marginal costs for product i are P_i, 3P_i, 5P_i, ... . Find a marginal-cost threshold t such that at least X marginal costs are <= t, then compute the minimum cost by taking all marginal costs below t and filling the remainder at cost t. Binary search the answer using this feasibility check.
