Classify indices into forced 1→0 removals (D), forced 0→1 additions (U), same-1 optional toggle indices (E), and ignore same-0 indices. Prove the optimal operation order consists of all removals before all additions, and that any chosen E index is flipped off during removals and back on during additions. For a fixed subset of E, removals should be ordered by descending C, additions by ascending C. Show the optimal chosen E subset is a prefix of E sorted by descending C, so evaluate all prefix sizes. Use Fenwick trees to maintain the rank-sum costs of the current removal and addition phases incrementally.
