Build a trie over all words to compute, for every prefix, how many words have that prefix. For each removed word, a prefix remains usable with k strings if its original count is at least k and the removed word does not have that prefix, or if its original count is at least k + 1 and the removed word does have that prefix. For each word, compute the longest prefix of that word whose count is at least k + 1, and separately determine the longest globally valid prefix that is not a prefix of that word. The answer for each index is the maximum of these two values, with 0 when fewer than k strings remain.
