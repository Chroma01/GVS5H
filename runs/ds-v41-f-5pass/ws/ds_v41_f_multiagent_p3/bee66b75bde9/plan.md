The valid black cells form a Young diagram anchored at the top-left: if a cell is black, every cell weakly above and weakly left of it must also be black. Therefore the configuration is impossible exactly when some white cell is weakly above-left of some black cell. This becomes a 2D dominance check: for each black cell \((X,Y)\), detect whether any white cell has row \(\le X\) and column \(\le Y\). With \(M \le 2 \times 10^5\), sort by row and sweep while maintaining the minimum column among processed white cells. Output Yes if no black cell violates the condition, otherwise No.
