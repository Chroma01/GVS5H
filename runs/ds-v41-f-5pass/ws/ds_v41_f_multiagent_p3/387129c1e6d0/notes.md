- **Problem core:** Simulate M axis-aligned moves; the final position is just the running coordinate. House counting requires finding all houses lying on any segment, inclusive of endpoints, counted distinctly.
- **Reduction:** A horizontal move at fixed y hits only houses with that y and x in the inclusive x-interval. A vertical move at fixed x hits only houses with that x and y in the inclusive y-interval. Each segment becomes a 1D interval on a coordinate line.
- **Offline interval union:** During simulation, append horizontal intervals to a dict keyed by y and vertical intervals to a dict keyed by x. After simulation, sort and merge intervals per key. A house is counted if its x lies in a merged horizontal interval for its y, or its y lies in a merged vertical interval for its x.
- **Merge rule:** Merge when next left <= current right + 1. Coalescing adjacent integer intervals is safe because all house coordinates are integers; it cannot introduce a false positive for an integer query point.
- **Query:** For a key's merged sorted interval list, use bisect_right(list, (val, INF)) - 1 to find the rightmost interval with left <= val, then test right >= val.
- **Correctness:** Every visited house lies on at least one traversed segment, so it appears in the relevant per-line interval. Conversely, every house satisfying a query lies on a traversed segment. Checking each house once and OR-ing the two orientations counts distinct houses exactly.
- **Complexity:** O((N + M) log M) time and O(N + M) memory. Python integers safely handle coordinates and path lengths up to about 2e14.
- **Edge cases:** Endpoints are inclusive; a turn point may belong to both a horizontal and vertical segment but is counted once; the initial point has no house, but later segment starts may be houses and are already covered by previous intervals.
- **Implementation details:** Use sys.stdin.buffer.read().split() for fast input. Direction tokens are bytes, compared with b'L', b'R', b'D', b'U'. INF = 10**30 is safely larger than any possible coordinate.
