We need construct, for each N, a modulus M and base A whose multiplicative order modulo M is exactly N. The key is to choose M as a divisor or product derived from a prime power, and A as a corresponding residue, so that the order is controlled without factoring huge N unnecessarily. Since N <= 1e9, we can use known identities and simple constructions based on N itself or its factors, ensuring A and M stay within 1e18. The implementation must be O(log N) or O(sqrt N) per test case at worst, handle T up to 1e4, and emit any valid pair.
