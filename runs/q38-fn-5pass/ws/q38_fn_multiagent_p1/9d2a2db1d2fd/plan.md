For a fixed route, the only relevant quantity is the minimum building height B along the route: if B is at least min(Y,Z), Takahashi can cross the whole route at floor min(Y,Z) and pay only the absolute difference between Y and Z; otherwise he must descend to B and later ascend to Z, paying Y + Z - 2B. Thus each query reduces to finding the maximum possible bottleneck B between the two cells in a grid graph whose edge weight is the minimum of the two endpoint building heights. Build a maximum spanning forest with Kruskal in descending edge weight and encode it as a Kruskal reconstruction tree, where the LCA of two original cells has weight equal to their maximum bottleneck. Preprocess this tree for LCA queries and apply the closed-form answer to every query. Use fast I/O and compact arrays because N=250k, E≈500k, and Q=2e5.
