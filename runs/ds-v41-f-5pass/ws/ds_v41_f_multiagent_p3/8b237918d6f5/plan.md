Binary search the answer `d` from `0` to `N-1`. For a fixed `d`, the condition that every 1→N path uses at least `d` chosen edges is equivalent to the existence of a labeling `h(v) ∈ {0..d}` with `h(1)=0`, `h(N)=d`, and `h(v) ≤ h(u)+1` for every edge `u→v`, where the number of edges with `h(v)=h(u)+1` is at most `K`. This feasibility check can be reduced to a minimum s-t cut on a layered graph with nodes `(v,i)` for `v=1..N` and `i=0..d+1`. Infinite-capacity edges enforce monotonicity and the constraint `h(v) ≤ h(u)+1`, while unit-capacity edges charge 1 exactly when `h(v)=h(u)+1`. If the min cut is at most `K`, then `d` is achievable.
