Recognize that adjacent swaps preserve the number and relative order of 1s, so the problem reduces to choosing a final contiguous block for those 1s. Compute the positions of all 1s, then find the minimum total distance to shift them into consecutive positions. This is a classic median-based optimization: after transforming positions by subtracting their index among the 1s, the optimal target is the median. Implement in O(N) time and O(number of 1s) memory to handle N up to 5e5.
