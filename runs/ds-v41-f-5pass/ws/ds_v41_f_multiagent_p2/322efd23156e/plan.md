Binary search on the answer M, the guaranteed minimum intake across all three vitamins. For a fixed M, compute the minimum calories needed to obtain at least M units of each vitamin independently from its own food group. Precompute for each vitamin type a DP over calorie budgets 0..X that gives the maximum amount of that vitamin obtainable. Sum the three minimal calorie costs and check if the total is ≤ X. The maximum feasible M is the answer.
