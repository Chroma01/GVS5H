Use a digit-DP function count_up_to(X) to count beautiful numbers in [1,X], then return count_up_to(r) - count_up_to(l-1). Numbers containing digit 0 are automatically beautiful because their digit product is 0. For zero-free numbers, the digit sum is at most 81, so track the sum and the capped prime exponents of the digit product for primes 2,3,5,7. At the end of each zero-free number, check whether the product exponents cover the factorization of its digit sum. Memoize the DP state over position, tightness, started flag, zero flag, digit sum, and capped exponents.
