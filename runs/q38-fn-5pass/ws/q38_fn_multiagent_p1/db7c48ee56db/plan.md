Use the symmetry of XOR: if K > N/2, choose the N-K excluded elements instead, since XOR(chosen) = XOR(all) XOR XOR(excluded). Let M = min(K, N-K); then C(N, M) <= 10^6 and M is small enough to enumerate all M-element subsets. For each subset, compute its XOR and update the maximum, either directly if K <= N/2 or as total_xor XOR subset_xor if K > N/2. Handle M = 0 and M = 1 as simple special cases for speed. This gives an O(C(N, M) * M) solution, comfortably within limits.
