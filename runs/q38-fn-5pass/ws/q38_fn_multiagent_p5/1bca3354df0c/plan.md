The graph is bipartite, so color each connected component and record the two color-class sizes. The winner can be decided from a small set of parity/count invariants: for odd N every complete play has parity M, while for even N the outcome depends on the number K of odd-sized components, the number z of isolated vertices, the parity of M, and the parity F of missing internal edges, where F = (sum(a_i*b_i) - M) mod 2. For even N, let r = K/2; Takahashi wins exactly when either (M is even and z <= K-3) or (z == K and F == r mod 2), otherwise Aoki wins. Implement component coloring and these counts in O(N+M), then apply the decision rule.
