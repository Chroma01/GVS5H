Reduce each query to finding the largest divisor d of A_i such that at least K elements of A are multiples of d. Precompute frequency of values and divisor-multiple counts up to max(A). Then process candidate d in descending order, assigning d as the answer for all values x that are multiples of d and not yet assigned. Map each original A_i to its value's assigned answer. This yields O(M log M + N) with M ≤ 10^6 and fits large N.
