In an optimal sequence, every 1→0 flip can be moved before every 0→1 flip, except for useless same-index positive/negative pairs that can be deleted; within 1→0 flips, larger C_i should be done first, and within 0→1 flips, smaller C_i should be done first. Forced mismatches are flipped once, while a matched 1 may be optionally flipped off before the positive phase and back on afterward. For any fixed number k of such optional pairs, it is optimal to choose the k largest C_i among positions with A_i=B_i=1. For a chosen k, the total cost can be computed from the negative multiset (forced 1→0 plus chosen matched 1s) and the positive multiset (forced 0→1 plus chosen matched 1s) using the sums of prefix sums of their sorted orders. We can start from k=0 and insert matched-1 values in descending order, updating these aggregates and the total cost, then take the minimum over all k.
