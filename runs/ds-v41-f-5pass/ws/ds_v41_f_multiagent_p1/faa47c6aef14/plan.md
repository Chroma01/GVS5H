Model red and blue ball movements as independent walks along the permutation cycles of P and Q containing X. Feasibility requires every red ball to lie on the P-cycle of X and every blue ball on the Q-cycle of X. For each color, the required operations form a contiguous reversed segment of the cycle starting from the farthest ball to X down to the predecessor of X. The minimum number of operations is the length of the shortest common supersequence of these two segments, which equals |R| + |B| - LCS(R, B). Compute LCS efficiently by mapping R to positions and finding the LIS of B's mapped elements.
