The minimax distance f(x,y) is the edge weight at which x and y first become connected when edges are added in nondecreasing order, equivalently the maximum edge on the MST path. Therefore, process all edges sorted by weight with DSU, maintaining for each component the numbers of still-unmatched A occurrences and B occurrences after optimally matching inside the component. When two components merge at weight w, every unmatched A in one and unmatched B in the other must have f=w, so match as many as possible immediately and add w times that count. Keep only the net unmatched type in the merged component; because future weights are no smaller, this greedy is optimal. At the end all K A and K B are matched, and the accumulated sum is the answer.
