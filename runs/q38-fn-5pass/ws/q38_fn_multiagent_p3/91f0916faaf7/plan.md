Reduce the condition \(f(S_i/S_{i+1})=A_i\) prime-by-prime: for each prime \(p\), if \(e_i=v_p(S_i)\), then \(|e_i-e_{i+1}|=v_p(A_i)\). The gcd condition is equivalent to \(\min_i e_i=0\) for every prime, and the score factorizes as \(\prod_p p^{\sum_i e_i}\). Thus the total answer is the product over primes of independent per-prime sums over nonnegative exponent walks with fixed absolute steps that touch zero. For each prime, do a DP over current exponent and whether zero has been touched, with exponent range bounded by the sum of that prime’s exponents in all \(A_i\).
