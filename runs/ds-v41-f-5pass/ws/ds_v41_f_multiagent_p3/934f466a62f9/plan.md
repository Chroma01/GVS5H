We reduce the problem to selecting 2K items and assigning each to one of three colors (X, Y, Z) such that each color's count is even, maximizing the sum of the chosen coordinate. The unconstrained optimal is the sum of the top 2K `max(X,Y,Z)` values. If a max‑color assignment can already achieve even parity, that sum is the answer. Otherwise, we need to fix the parity of two odd colors using at most two reassignments or replacements (swaps with unselected items). We compute the cheapest such operations via precomputed best candidates for each directed color transfer, then take the minimum loss over all reachable parity states.
