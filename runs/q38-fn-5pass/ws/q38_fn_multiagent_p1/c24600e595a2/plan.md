Classify indices into P = positions with A_i=1,B_i=0, Q = positions with A_i=0,B_i=1, and R = positions with A_i=B_i=1. In an optimal sequence, P and Q are flipped once, R may be flipped off then on at most once, and all off-flips occur before all on-flips; off-flips are sorted by decreasing C and on-flips by increasing C. For a fixed number s of R indices to toggle extra, the best choice is the s largest weights in R. Iterate s from 0 to |R|, maintaining the cost of the off list P ∪ selected_R and the on list Q ∪ selected_R, and take the minimum total cost.
