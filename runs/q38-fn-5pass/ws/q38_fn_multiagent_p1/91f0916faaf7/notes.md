- **Prime-wise reduction:** For each prime p, let h_i be the exponent of p in S_i and e_i be the exponent of p in A_i. The condition f(S_i/S_{i+1})=A_i is exactly |h_i-h_{i+1}|=e_i. The gcd condition is min_i h_i=0 for every prime. The score factorizes as product over primes of p^{sum_i h_i}, so the final answer is the product over primes of their independent weighted path sums. Primes absent from all A_i force all h_i=0 and contribute 1.
- **First-zero decomposition:** For a fixed prime, every valid path has a unique first index k with h_k=0. The prefix before k is strictly positive and must end at height e_{k-1} (unless k=1). The suffix from k is nonnegative and starts at 0. The per-prime sum is sum_k pref[k]*suff[k], where pref[k] is the weighted sum of positive prefixes ending at k-1 with height e_{k-1}, and suff[k] is the weighted sum of nonnegative suffixes starting at k with height 0.
- **Suffix DP:** Define dp_i[h] as the sum of p^{sum_{j=i}^N h_j} over nonnegative continuations from position i with current height h. Base dp_N[h]=p^h. For step e_i, dp_i[h]=p^h*(dp_{i+1}[h+e_i] plus dp_{i+1}[h-e_i] if h>=e_i), with only one term when e_i=0. suff[i]=dp_i[0]. Compute backwards. At position i, only heights up to the sum of exponents before i can be reached from an earlier first zero, so a dynamic cap keeps work small.
- **Prefix DP:** Define pos_i[h] for h>0 as the weighted sum of strictly positive prefixes ending at position i with height h. Base pos_1[h]=p^h. Transition is analogous, but previous height 0 is disallowed; in particular, when current height equals the step, the transition from height 0 is omitted. pref[1]=1, and for k>1, pref[k]=pos_{k-1}[e_{k-1}] if e_{k-1}>0, otherwise 0. Compute forward, capping heights by the remaining exponent sum to the right because a larger height can never reach zero later.
- **Zero steps and bounds:** A zero exponent step forces equal adjacent heights and must be treated as one transition, not two. It also means a first zero cannot occur immediately after a positive height, so pref[k]=0 when e_{k-1}=0. For any path containing a zero, every height is at most E=sum_i e_i. Dynamic caps make total inner iterations over all primes about (N-1)*sum_p E_p. Since A_i<=1000, sum_p E_p is at most about 9(N-1), so the total is around 9e6.
- **Implementation details:** Factor all A_i using a smallest-prime-factor sieve, storing one exponent array per prime. Precompute p^h modulo 998244353 up to E. Use in-place updates for zero steps and shifted new arrays for positive steps. Multiply per-prime totals modulo 998244353. If no prime appears, all A_i are 1 and the answer is 1.
