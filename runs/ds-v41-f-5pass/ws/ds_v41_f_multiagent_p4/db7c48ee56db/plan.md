Exploit the guarantee \(\binom{N}{K} \le 10^6\): the number of valid selections is small enough for exhaustive enumeration. If \(K \le N-K\), enumerate all \(K\)-subsets directly; otherwise enumerate all \((N-K)\)-subsets as exclusions and XOR them against the total XOR. Maintain the running XOR during recursive subset generation so each leaf is evaluated in O(1). Track the maximum XOR over all valid selections. This yields at most about \(10^6\) leaves with small depth, easily within limits.
