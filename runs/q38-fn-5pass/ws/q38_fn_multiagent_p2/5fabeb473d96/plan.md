Model the required sum as the total number of recursive trees, `(N-1)!`, times the expected distance, and decompose the distance by edges. Edge `i` contributes exactly when one of the two query vertices lies in the subtree of `i` and the other does not. Using standard random recursive tree properties, derive closed-form probabilities for the possible label ranges of `i` relative to `u` and `v`. Reduce each query to a constant number of prefix sums over weighted coefficients, after precomputing factorials and modular inverses. This gives `O(N + Q)` total time.
