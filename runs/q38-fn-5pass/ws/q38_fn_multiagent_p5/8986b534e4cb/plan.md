Model each person by the undirected town interval `[l,r]` and a direction (`l->r` or `r->l`). Prove that a set of people is feasible iff it contains no forbidden pair: (1) two same-direction intervals strictly cross, or (2) two intervals share an endpoint and their other endpoints lie on the same side of that endpoint. Then compute, for every person `i`, the smallest later index `j` that forms a forbidden pair with `i` by scanning people left-to-right and maintaining only “active” people that have not yet received a bad partner. Active same-direction intervals are laminar, so shared-endpoint conflicts can be handled by endpoint/side buckets, and crossing conflicts can be found and removed with two segment trees (one per direction) storing maximum right endpoint by left endpoint. Finally, suffix-minimize these earliest bad partners to answer each query interval in O(1).
