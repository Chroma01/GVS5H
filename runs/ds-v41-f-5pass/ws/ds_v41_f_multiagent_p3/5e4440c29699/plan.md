Model the town as the full (W+1)×(H+1) grid with a rectangular hole [L,R]×[D,U]. Count all monotone right/up paths by summing DP values over valid cells. In the full grid, the number of paths ending at (x,y) has the closed form C(x+y+2, x+1)−1. Split the board into four regions around the hole: left, below, above, and right. For the left and below regions the DP is unchanged, while for the above and right regions we subtract paths that leave the hole through its top or right boundary. Use hockey-stick identities to reduce rectangle and boundary sums to O(1) formulas plus at most O(W+H) loop iterations.
