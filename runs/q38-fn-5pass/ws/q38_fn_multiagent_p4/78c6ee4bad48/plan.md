Convert the state to gaps between consecutive pieces. Show that one operation on four consecutive pieces reverses the three corresponding gaps, equivalently swaps gap positions `i` and `i+2`. Therefore each gap can move only within its index parity, and any permutation within odd-indexed gaps and even-indexed gaps is reachable. The sum of coordinates is `N*x_1 + sum((N-k)*g_k)`, where `x_1` is fixed and the weights `N-k` decrease with `k`. For each parity class, minimize the weighted sum by assigning smaller gaps to larger weights, i.e. sort that class’s gaps ascending and place them in increasing index order. Compute the resulting minimum sum in `O(N log N)` time.
