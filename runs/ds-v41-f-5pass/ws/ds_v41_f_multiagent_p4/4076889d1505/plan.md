Reduce the condition to finding a modulus M such that the multiplicative order of A modulo M equals N. Factor N into pairwise coprime prime powers q_i = p_i^{e_i}. For each q_i, find a distinct prime r_i ≡ 1 mod q_i and an element A_i of order q_i modulo r_i using the fact that the multiplicative group mod r_i is cyclic. Combine the pairs (A_i, r_i) via the Chinese Remainder Theorem to obtain A and M = ∏ r_i, whose multiplicative order is the lcm of q_i, i.e., N. The small size of N (≤10^9) guarantees that the chosen primes r_i and their product M stay well below 10^18.
