For a fixed subarray, the minimum increments needed to make it non-decreasing is the sum of its running prefix maxima minus the sum of its elements. For a fixed left endpoint, the running maximum changes only at indices obtained by repeatedly jumping to the next strictly greater element to the right. Precompute next-greater pointers and prefix sums, then use binary lifting to compute the cost of any subarray `[l, r]` in `O(log n)` by summing full contributions of record maxima before the last record and adding the last record’s partial contribution. Since this cost is monotone non-increasing as `l` moves right, use a two-pointer scan over the right endpoint to count all feasible starts.
