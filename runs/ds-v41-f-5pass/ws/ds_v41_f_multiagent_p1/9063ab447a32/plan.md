The total cost for buying k_i units of product i is sum P_i*k_i^2. Increasing from k_i-1 to k_i units adds marginal cost P_i*(2k_i-1), so for a fixed total K the cheapest purchase is the sum of the K smallest marginal costs across all products. The answer is the largest K such that this minimum possible cost is at most M. Use binary search over K, and for each K compute the sum of the K smallest marginal costs using a threshold binary search with O(N) counting and summing formulas. This gives an efficient solution within the constraints.
