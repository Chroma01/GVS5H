Use the fact that buying the k-th unit of product i has marginal cost P_i(2k-1), so the problem becomes selecting the cheapest marginal increments under budget M. For a threshold X, compute in O(N) how many units of each product have marginal cost at most X via k_i = floor((floor(X/P_i)+1)/2), along with total cost sum P_i k_i^2 and total units sum k_i. Binary search the largest X such that this total cost is at most M. After that, all remaining affordable increments cost X+1 each, so add floor((M-cost)/(X+1)) to the total units. Use Python integers for large values and verify edge cases against brute force.
