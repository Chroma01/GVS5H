Model each additional unit of product `i` as a marginal cost `(2t - 1) * P_i` for the `t`-th unit. The optimal purchase for any budget is the prefix of the globally sorted marginal costs. Binary search the smallest marginal cost `X` such that buying all marginal costs `<= X` costs more than `M`; then all marginal costs `< X` are affordable, and the remaining budget can buy `floor((M - cost_less) / X)` units among those with marginal cost exactly `X`. For a threshold `x`, the number of units from product `i` is `k = ((x // P_i) + 1) // 2`, and its cost contribution is `k^2 * P_i`, capped during summation to avoid huge integers.
