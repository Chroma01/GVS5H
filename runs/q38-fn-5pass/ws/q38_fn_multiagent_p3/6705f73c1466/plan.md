Treat the 1s as indistinguishable but preserve their relative order, since adjacent swaps cannot reduce the total distance by crossing identical 1s. If the 1s are at positions p[0], p[1], ..., p[k-1], then moving them to a contiguous block starting at x costs sum |p[i] - (x+i)|. Rewrite this as sum |(p[i]-i) - x|, so the optimal x is the median of the adjusted positions p[i]-i. The answer is the sum of absolute deviations from that median, computable in O(N) time after scanning the string.
