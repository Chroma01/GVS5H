Reduce f(x) to the odd part of x, so the target is the sum of odd parts of all A_i+A_j for i<=j. Group pairs by the exact 2-adic valuation k of A_i+A_j, and for each k compute the number and total sum of such pairs using residues modulo 2^{k+1}. Since A_i <= 10^7, only k up to about 24 matters. Hash maps per modulus allow O(N log max A) total processing. Treat diagonal terms i=j separately using f(2A_i)=f(A_i). Sum (A_i+A_j)/2^k over each exact-valuation class to obtain the final answer.
