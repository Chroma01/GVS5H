Use linearity over each value x: its contribution is x times the sum, over all permutations, of 10 raised to the total digit length of the elements after x. For fixed x, write 10^{len_y}=1+(10^{len_y}-1) and expand; a subset S of other elements is entirely after x with probability 1/(|S|+1), so the needed factor is N! times sum_s e_s/(s+1), where e_s is the elementary symmetric sum of a_y=10^{len_y}-1. Since there are at most 6 digit lengths, form P(t)=prod_d (1+a_d t)^{c_d}; for removing one element of digit length d, we need the integral of P(t)/(1+a_d t). Compute all coefficients of P in O(6N) using the differential recurrence from R(t)P'(t)=B(t)P(t), then divide by each linear factor and combine with digit-group value sums.
