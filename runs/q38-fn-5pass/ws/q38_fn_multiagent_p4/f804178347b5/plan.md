Model the repeated majority operation as a complete ternary tree with the input bits as leaves and the final value as the root. For every node, compute two values: the minimum number of leaf flips needed to force that node to output 0, and the minimum needed to force it to output 1. For a leaf, these costs are 0 for its current value and 1 for the opposite value; for an internal node, a value is obtained if at least two of its three children output that value, so combine child costs by trying all choices where at least two children are forced to the desired value. Process the tree bottom-up from the leaves to the root, then output the cost at the root for the value opposite to its current value.
