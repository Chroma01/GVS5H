First establish the necessary and sufficient feasibility condition: R must be even, and if R=0 then B must also be even; otherwise every feasible case has a construction. Build simple cycles on a small grid and translate them into the 1..10^9 board, keeping all occupied squares distinct. Use separate generators for all-red cycles, all-blue cycles, mixed cycles with even B, and mixed cycles with odd B obtained by adding a one-blue detour to an outer red edge of the even-B/all-red base. Generate the pieces in cyclic order so each printed piece's color matches the move to the next printed position. Process all test cases in total O(sum(R+B)) time and output.
