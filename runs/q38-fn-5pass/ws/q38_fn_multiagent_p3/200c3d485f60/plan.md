Model the array by its n-1 adjacent transitions. Exactly k transitions must be equal, and the remaining n-1-k transitions must be different. Choose the k equal-transition positions in C(n-1, k) ways, choose the first element in m ways, and choose each different transition in m-1 ways. The final count is C(n-1, k) * m * (m-1)^(n-1-k) modulo 1e9+7. Implement modular exponentiation and modular combination efficiently for n up to 1e5.
