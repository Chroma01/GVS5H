Convert the sorted coordinates into adjacent gaps, since the global first coordinate never moves and the sum depends linearly on these gaps. Show that one operation on four consecutive pieces merely swaps the two gaps at distance 2, so the odd-indexed gaps and even-indexed gaps are independently permutable. The objective becomes minimizing a weighted sum of gaps, where gap j has weight N-j. Assign the smallest gaps in each parity class to the largest weights, i.e. sort each parity class increasingly along increasing gap index. Finally, reconstruct or directly compute the minimum coordinate sum in O(N log N) time.
