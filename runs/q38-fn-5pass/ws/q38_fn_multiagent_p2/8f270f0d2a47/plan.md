Use a digit DP to count beautiful numbers up to a bound, then answer the range as count(r) - count(l - 1). The DP will process decimal digits while tracking the current digit sum, current digit product, whether the number has started, and the tight constraint. Leading zeros must be ignored so they do not incorrectly make the product zero. At the end of a number, it is beautiful if the product is divisible by the sum, with product zero automatically satisfying the condition. Since r < 10^9, the number of digits is small enough for memoized DP with exact product or compact product representation.
