For any subarray, the minimum increments needed to make it non-decreasing is the sum of its prefix maxima minus the sum of its elements, because the greedy final value at each position is the maximum seen so far. Represent the prefix maxima of a subarray as a chain of “next strictly greater” record positions, then preprocess this chain with binary lifting so the sum of prefix maxima for any interval can be queried in logarithmic time. Since this cost is monotone when extending the right end and monotone when moving the left end rightward, use a two-pointer scan over the array. For each right endpoint, advance the left endpoint until the cost is at most k, then count all valid subarrays ending there. This yields an O(n log n) solution with O(n log n) preprocessing.
