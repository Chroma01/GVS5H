Model Takahashi's progress as a growing connected region; his strength equals the sum of original strengths of the cells in that region. Since absorbing any eligible boundary slime strictly increases his strength, greedily absorbing every currently eligible boundary cell cannot reduce future options. When no boundary cell satisfies S < current_strength / X, no further absorption is possible, so the final current strength is optimal. Maintain the boundary with a min-heap keyed by slime strength, absorbing the smallest eligible boundary cell and adding newly adjacent cells. The final sum is the answer, with complexity O(HW log(HW)).
