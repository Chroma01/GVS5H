Model the repeated majority operation as a complete ternary tree whose leaves are the characters of A and whose root is A'_1. For each node, compute two values: the minimum number of leaf flips needed to make that node output 0, and the minimum number needed to make it output 1. Leaves have costs 0/1 depending on the original bit, and an internal node’s costs are obtained by choosing child outputs so that at least two children agree with the desired majority value. Process the tree bottom-up level by level in O(3^N) time, then output the cost of the opposite value from the root’s original output.
