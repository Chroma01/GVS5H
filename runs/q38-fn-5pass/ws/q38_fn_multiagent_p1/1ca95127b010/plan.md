The operations only translate the block of Y ones by X positions and the block of X zeros by Y positions, so the ordered list of positions of 1s modulo X and the ordered list of positions of 0s modulo Y are invariant. For this block-swap rewriting system, these two ordered residue lists are also sufficient: if two strings have the same lists, the allowed operations can transform one into the other. Therefore, the decision problem reduces to computing a signature for S and T and checking equality. The signature naturally includes the counts of 1s and 0s through list lengths, and cases like X=1 or Y=1 are handled automatically.
