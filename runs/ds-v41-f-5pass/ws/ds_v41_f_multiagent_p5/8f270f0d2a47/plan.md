Use digit DP to count numbers not exceeding a bound, then answer with F(r)-F(l-1). Since the digit sum is at most 81, handle the divisibility condition by fixing each possible target digit sum s and counting numbers whose digit sum equals s and whose digit product is divisible by s. Track position, tight flag, started flag, current digit sum, and digit product modulo s; a zero digit naturally makes the product zero modulo s. Sum counts over all valid target sums. This avoids storing the full product and keeps the state space manageable.
