Root the tree arbitrarily and process it bottom-up. For each subtree, at most one incomplete path can connect to the parent, so each node returns either a closed state or the length of that open path. At a node, collect open lengths from children; more than two open children makes success impossible. If two open children exist, their lengths must sum to K-1 to close a path; if one exists, either close it when its length equals K-1 or pass it upward with length increased by one; if none exist, pass upward a length of 1 unless K=1. Finally, verify the root can close all remaining open paths without a parent. This yields a deterministic O(NK) algorithm.
