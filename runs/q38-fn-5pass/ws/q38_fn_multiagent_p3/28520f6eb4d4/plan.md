Treat an observer at height y as looking in slope coordinates; building i occupies the slope interval [ -y/X_i, (H_i-y)/X_i ]. Because the left endpoints increase with X_i, building i is completely hidden exactly when its top slope is not larger than the maximum top slope among earlier buildings. Therefore all buildings are visible iff the adjacent top slopes are strictly increasing. For each adjacent pair, the inequality (H_i-y)/X_i <= (H_{i-1}-y)/X_{i-1} gives one threshold T_i, and the answer is the largest nonnegative T_i, or -1 if all thresholds are negative. Compute thresholds as exact rationals and output with sufficient precision.
