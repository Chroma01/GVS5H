The key observation is that buying the x-th unit of product i has marginal cost P_i(2x+1), so the globally optimal way to buy K total units is to take the K smallest marginal costs across all products. To test whether K units are affordable, binary search a threshold V on marginal cost; for each product count how many marginal costs are ≤ V and sum their costs using the closed form. If the count reaches K, the exact minimum cost for K units is total_sum - (count - K) * V, which can be compared to M. The answer is then the maximum K for which this check succeeds. Use outer binary search on K with a safe upper bound.
