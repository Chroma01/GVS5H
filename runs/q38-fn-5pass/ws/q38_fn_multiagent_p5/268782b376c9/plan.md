Binary search the answer. For a candidate score x, convert each points[i] into a required visit count c_i = ceil(x / points[i]); x is feasible iff there is a walk of at most m moves starting at -1 that visits every index at least c_i times. Model the walk by traversal counts on the n-1 internal edges; if the final index is E, indices up to E receive one free visit from the initial rightward sweep, while indices after E require paired traversals. For fixed E this becomes minimizing a sum of nonnegative half-traversal variables under adjacent-sum lower bounds, which can be solved with prefix and suffix DP where each side’s optimal cost as a function of the shared boundary is of the form max(b + A, B). Combining these functions over all possible final indices gives the minimum moves for x in O(n), so the full solution runs in O(n log(max_score)).
