Use the guarantee \(\binom{N}{K} \le 10^6\) to enumerate all relevant subsets directly. If \(K \le N-K\), enumerate all \(K\)-element subsets and compute their XORs; otherwise enumerate all \((N-K)\)-element complements and compute the chosen XOR as \(\text{totalXor} \oplus \text{complementXor}\). Track the maximum XOR seen during enumeration. This keeps the work proportional to the smaller side of the binomial coefficient, which is at most about \(10^6\) combinations and small subset sizes.
