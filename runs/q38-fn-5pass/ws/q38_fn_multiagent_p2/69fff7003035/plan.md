Use linearity over numbers: each number x contributes x multiplied by 10 raised to the total digit length of the numbers placed after it. For a fixed x, summing over all permutations gives a multiplier that depends only on the digit length of x, via subsets of the other numbers. Group numbers by decimal length, and use a generating function whose coefficient of z^k is the sum of 10^{total length} over subsets of size k. Compute the base product coefficients efficiently with an O(LN) recurrence, divide out the factor corresponding to the chosen digit length, and combine with the sum of numbers in that length group.
