- **Model:** Removing adjacent pairs repeatedly = building a non-crossing matching on positions. Each matched pair (i,j) contributes a signed term +A_p - A_q (abs = max of the two signings). N even => N/2 plus and N/2 minus signs; N odd => (N-1)/2 of each plus one unpaired element contributing 0.
- **Order independence:** For an even-length contiguous block any balanced sign assignment is realizable: while nonempty, a sign change exists, so some adjacent (+,-) pair exists; remove it; repeat. Hence a block's value depends only on its multiset (= sum of larger half minus sum of smaller half). This is why the sample works and why brute choices coincide.
- **Unpaired position parity:** The unpaired element is never removed, so no arc can span over it (spanning needs the skipped element already gone). Thus it splits the sequence into two fully matched even-length parts => its 1-indexed position is odd. Consequence: the "just drop the median value" idea is WRONG (e.g. N=3, A=(1,2,3): median 2 sits at even index 2, true answer 1). Also (1,2,100): dropping value 2 would give 99 but the answer is 98.
- **Correct reduction:** Even N: answer = sum(largest N/2) - sum(smallest N/2) over all values (order irrelevant). Odd N: answer = max over odd 1-indexed k of value(A[0:k-1]) + value(A[k:n]), where each part is an even-length contiguous block scored by the half-sum rule; A[k-1] is discarded. Parts are solved independently (no cross pairing possible), so you cannot just sort the remaining multiset.
- **Data structure:** Need value(prefix of even length) for all even lengths, and value(suffix of even length) for all even lengths. Use the two-heap "running lower half" method: max-heap `lo` keeps the smallest half (target size i/2 at even i), min-heap `hi` the rest; keep sum of `lo`. Value = total - 2*sum_lo. heappush/heappop are C-implemented, faster and simpler than a Fenwick with binary lifting. Invariant len(lo)-len(hi) in {0,1} forces len(lo)=i/2 at even i.
- **Suffix trick:** Compute prefix values on the reversed array; prefr[t] equals value of the last t original elements.
- **Combination (odd N):** 0-based prefix length e is even; separator is index e; suffix length n-e-1. Answer = max over e=0,2,...,n-1 of pref[e] + prefr[n-e-1]. Verified on sample 2: pref[0]+prefr[6]=0+14=14 is the max.
- **Verified samples:** (1,2,5,3)->5; (3,1,4,1,5,9,2)->14; all-ones N=5 ->0. Extra checks: (1,2,3)->1, (1,2,100)->98, (1,100,2,100,3)->197.
- **Complexity:** O(N log N) time, O(N) memory; sums up to 3e14 fit Python ints (no overflow worry). Handle i only when even; edges: N=2 gives |a0-a1|, N=3 iterates e=0 and e=2.
