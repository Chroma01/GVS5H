Use prefix sums for nums and cost so any subarray cost is expressed via prefix differences. Define DP over the first i elements and the number of subarrays already formed; the transition chooses the last cut position and adds its cost. The direct transition is O(n^3), which is too slow for n=1000, so optimize each DP layer with convex hull trick/Li Chao since the minimization is over lines in the cut prefix-cost. Then take the minimum over all possible numbers of subarrays ending at n. Validate against the examples and brute force on small cases.
