The problem reduces to constructing a simple cycle on the grid with exactly R orthogonal edges (red) and B diagonal edges (blue), where each vertex's color equals its outgoing edge type. Necessary conditions are R even and, if R=0, B even; these are also sufficient. For R=0, use a diamond cycle for even B≥4 and a special case for B=2. For R>0 even, handle B=0 with a rectangle (or adjacent pair for R=2) and B>0 with a parametric corner-staircase construction (separate formulas for even/odd B). The construction runs in O(R+B) per test case and uses a large coordinate offset to keep positions positive.
