Reduce each element’s required number of divisions by 4 to a cost c(x), where c(x) is the smallest d with floor(x / 4^d) = 0. For a query [l, r], the total required “division tokens” is S = sum c(x) over x in [l, r], and the largest single-element cost is c(r) because c(x) is nondecreasing. Since each operation can consume at most two tokens and the most demanding element must be selected c(r) times, the query answer is max(ceil(S / 2), c(r)). Compute S efficiently by grouping numbers into intervals where c(x) is constant, then sum answers over all queries.
