The answer for each position i is the largest divisor d of A_i such that at least K elements of A are divisible by d. Build a frequency array of values up to max(A), then for every possible d compute how many array elements are multiples of d by summing frequencies over multiples of d. For every d whose multiple-count is at least K, update all multiples of d with d as a candidate answer; processing d in increasing order and overwriting, or in decreasing order and setting only unset entries, leaves the maximum valid divisor for each value. Finally, output the stored best divisor for each original A_i. This gives O(M log M) time for M = max(A_i) <= 10^6 and O(N + M) memory.
