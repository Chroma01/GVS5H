The key observation is that the number of ways to choose K elements, C(N,K), is at most 10^6. This allows direct enumeration of all K-element subsets without needing advanced data structures. For each subset, we compute the XOR of its elements and track the maximum. Special-casing K=1 provides a small optimization, but full enumeration remains feasible because when K is large, N is necessarily small. The total work is bounded by O(C(N,K) * K), which is comfortably within limits for Python.
