Fix each index as the middle of a size-5 subsequence and count choices of two indices before it and two after it. For a fixed middle value x, the middle value is a unique mode iff either at least two of the four side elements equal x, or exactly one side element equals x and the remaining three side values are all distinct. Maintain left and right frequency counts while sweeping the middle index, along with sums of C(count, 2), to compute pair categories and distinct non-x pair counts efficiently. For each middle index, add the valid combinations modulo 10^9 + 7.
