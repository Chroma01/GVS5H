First verify that every red ball can reach box X along the P-cycle containing X, and every blue ball can reach X along the Q-cycle containing X; otherwise the answer is -1. For each color, all required movements reduce to the path from the farthest initial ball of that color to X, because all other paths on the same permutation cycle are suffixes of that farthest path. This gives two operation sequences R and B, excluding X. The minimum number of operations is the shortest common supersequence length of R and B, which equals |R| + |B| - LCS(R, B). Since each sequence has distinct box indices, compute the LCS by mapping positions in R and taking the LIS of matching positions in B.
