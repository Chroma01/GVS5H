Process the M moves online while maintaining Santa’s current position. The main challenge is counting each house at most once when it lies on a horizontal or vertical segment. Group houses by their x-coordinate for vertical moves and by their y-coordinate for horizontal moves, then support range queries that remove found houses from all relevant groups. Since each house can be removed once, an efficient skip structure such as DSU/next-pointer per coordinate group gives near O((N+M) log N) total time. Finally, output the ending position and the distinct count.
