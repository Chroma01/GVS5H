Model the repeated majority operation as a complete ternary tree of depth N, where leaves are the characters of A and each internal node is the majority of its three children. Compute, for every node, both its current majority value and the minimum number of leaf flips needed to force that node to output 0 or 1. Combine child costs by choosing the cheapest way to make at least two children output the desired value. The answer is the cost to force the root to the value opposite its current value.
