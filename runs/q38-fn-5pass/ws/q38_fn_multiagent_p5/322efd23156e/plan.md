Treat the three vitamin types independently because each food contributes to only one vitamin. For each vitamin, compute a 0/1 knapsack DP over calories up to X, where dp[c] is the maximum amount of that vitamin obtainable with at most c calories. Then binary search the answer T: a target T is feasible if the minimum calories needed to reach at least T in vitamin 1, vitamin 2, and vitamin 3 sum to at most X. The maximum feasible T is the required answer.
