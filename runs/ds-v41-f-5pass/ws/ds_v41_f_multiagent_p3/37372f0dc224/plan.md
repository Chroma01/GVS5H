The shortest palindrome with prefix S is obtained by finding the longest palindromic suffix of S. Removing that suffix leaves a prefix of length k, and the answer is S followed by the reverse of that prefix. This reduces to finding the longest suffix of S that matches a prefix of reverse(S). Use the KMP prefix function on reverse(S) + '#' + S. Then k = |S| - L, and the answer is S + reverse(S[0:k]). This runs in O(|S|) time and memory, suitable for n up to 500,000.
