We need to minimize the total cost of flipping bits to transform sequence A into B. The operation cost depends on the current sum of C_i for bits equal to 1. The key insight is that all 1→0 flips should be done first (in decreasing order of C), followed by all 0→1 flips (in increasing order of C). Indices where A_i = B_i = 1 are optional: we may either leave them untouched or flip them off and back on, which effectively removes them from the cost during the middle operations. The decision reduces to choosing a suffix of optional indices (sorted by C) to turn off. We can compute the total cost for every possible suffix length in O(N log N) using prefix sums and binary search, then take the minimum. The implementation must handle ties in C correctly by adding/subtracting tie-breaking constants for mandatory flips.
