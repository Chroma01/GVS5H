Characterize a valid connected graph by its BFS layers from vertex 1: layer 0 is {1}, layer sizes are positive, parity of layer index determines even/odd distance, edges are allowed only within a layer or between adjacent layers, and every vertex in layer i>0 must have at least one neighbor in layer i-1. For fixed consecutive layer sizes a,b, the edge-count generating factor for adding the new layer is C(rem,b)(1+x)^{C(b,2)}((1+x)^a-1)^b, where rem is the number of unused labels. Summing over ordered layer sizes with total K=N/2 even and K odd vertices gives the desired generating polynomial in x. To avoid costly polynomial convolutions, evaluate the whole DP at D+1 points x=0,...,D, where D=N(N-1)/2, and interpolate the answer polynomial modulo P. The DP state tracks used even/odd counts, previous layer size, and next parity, with transitions adding one BFS layer. This produces all coefficients for M=N-1,...,D.
