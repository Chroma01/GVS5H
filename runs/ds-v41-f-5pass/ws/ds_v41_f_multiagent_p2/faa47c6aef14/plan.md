Model red and blue ball movements independently along the permutations P and Q. For each color, determine whether every initially occupied box can reach X; if not, answer is -1. The required operations for one color form a contiguous ordered chain on the permutation cycle ending just before X, from the farthest occupied box to X’s predecessor. The joint minimum is the shortest common supersequence of the red and blue operation sequences, which reduces to |R| + |B| - LCS(R,B). Since both sequences have distinct boxes, compute their LCS efficiently using LIS after position mapping.
