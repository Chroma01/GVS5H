Collect the 0-based positions of all `1` characters in the string. If there are `k` ones, the final contiguous block of ones must occupy positions `x, x+1, ..., x+k-1` for some integer `x`. To minimize adjacent swaps, match the `j`-th one in order to the `j`-th target position, so the cost for a chosen `x` is the sum of absolute differences between original positions and target positions. This reduces to minimizing `sum |(pos[j] - j) - x|`, whose optimal `x` is the median of the transformed values `pos[j] - j`. Compute that median and the corresponding minimum total cost in linear time after collecting positions.
