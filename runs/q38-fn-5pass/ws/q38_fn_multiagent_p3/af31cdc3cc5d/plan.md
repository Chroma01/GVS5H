Model the operations as a non-crossing matching of positions. For any even-length segment, the maximum score depends only on its multiset: choose the largest half as positive and the smallest half as negative, giving `sum_largest - sum_smallest`, and this is feasible by repeatedly pairing adjacent opposite signs. For odd `N`, exactly one element remains; it cannot lie inside any pair, so its 1-based index must be odd and it splits the array into two independent even segments. Therefore compute the even-segment value for every even prefix and suffix, then maximize their sum over odd split positions. Use two heaps/multisets to maintain the lower and upper halves while scanning, yielding an `O(N log N)` solution.
