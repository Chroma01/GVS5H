Model the pieces as an ordered pair of distinct vertices, but avoid explicit BFS on the O(N^2) configuration graph. Derive a compressed characterization of optimal sequences: the pieces move along shortest paths to a “passing place” where their order can be exchanged, and this passing place is either a vertex with available incident detours (branching) or a cycle/2-connected block. Compute distances from S and T, local detour/cycle costs, and the cost of swapping at each possible passing place; take the minimum, with -1 only when the graph is a simple path. Implement the resulting shortest-path/minimization computation in O((N+M) log N) or better.
