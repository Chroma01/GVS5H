- **Objective:** f(x,y) = minimax (bottleneck) path weight. Minimize sum over a bijection between multiset A and multiset B.
- **Key identity:** f(x,y) = weight of the LCA of leaves x,y in the Kruskal reconstruction tree. Equivalent: sort edges ascending; when two DSU components first join at weight w, every cross pair has f-value exactly w.
- **Reduction:** minimum-cost matching between A-tokens and B-tokens on the merge hierarchy; a pair matched when its components merge pays that merge weight.
- **Algorithm (final, unchanged):** sort edges ascending; maintain per-component signed surplus = (#A)-(#B). For edge (w,u,v) with roots ru!=rv, if surpluses have opposite signs, match m=min(|s_ru|,|s_rv|) pairs adding m*w, then union with surplus s_ru+s_rv. Total surplus ends at 0 since |A|=|B|=K. Output accumulated sum.
- **Correctness:** at a merge, any pair split across the two components has cost >= w (later merges are >= w), so matching the maximum possible now at cost w is optimal; leftover tokens carry up and only ever match at >= this weight. Binary merge = internal node of Kruskal tree; matching max across its two children is the optimal tree DP.
- **Equal weights:** order within a same-weight group is irrelevant — total pairs matched at weight w equals (sum|s_i| - |sum s_i|)/2 over the group, independent of merge order. Verified by hand (chain 2,-1,-1 gives 2 either order).
- **Duplicates / overlaps:** multiplicities in cnt[] handle repeated A/B entries. Overlap (a vertex in both A and B) is handled too (its +1 and -1 cancel); the algorithm does not rely on the A_i != B_j guarantee. Problem guarantee (A_i != B_j) just means |A|,|B| pools are disjoint.
- **Validation (this task):** built a self-test (`--selftest`) that generates 5000 random connected graphs with N in [2,7], random weights from small sets (1,2,3,5) to force ties plus occasional 1e9, K in [1,N] with heavy duplication, A and B sampled from disjoint vertex pools (respecting the A_i != B_j guarantee). Full f-matrix computed by bottleneck Floyd-Warshall; true min matching by permutation enumeration (K<=7). Result: **PASS**, greedy == brute on all 5000 cases (seed 12345), no counterexample found.
- **Verification:** hand-traced both provided samples: output 8 and 3, matching expected.
- **Pitfalls:** not a sorted-order greedy — uneven subtree counts require the signed-surplus postponement. Sums up to ~2e5*1e9 fit Python int. DSU uses iterative find with path halving.
- **Complexity:** O(M log M) sort + near-linear DSU; N,M,K <= 2e5.
