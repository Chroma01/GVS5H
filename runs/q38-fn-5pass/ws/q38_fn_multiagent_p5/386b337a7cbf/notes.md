- **Unified state:** `dp0[p]` and `dp1[p]` are Python-int bitsets of alternating sums for non-empty subsequences with even/odd length and product category `p`. Categories are `0`, exact products `1..limit`, and `OVER = limit + 1` for positive products above `limit`. The empty subsequence is never stored.
- **Why OVER is needed:** A positive product above `limit` cannot be an answer, but it can later be multiplied by a zero and become valid product `0`. Pruning such states would miss cases like `nums=[2,0], k=2, limit=1`, where `[2,0]` has product `0` and sum `2`.
- **Positive transitions:** Skipping copies states. Appending positive `x`: product `0` stays `0`; exact `p` maps to `p*x` if `<= limit`, otherwise `OVER`; `OVER` stays `OVER`. Parity flips, sum adds `x` from even length and subtracts `x` from odd length. Starting `[x]` creates product `x` or `OVER`, odd length, sum `x`.
- **Zero transitions:** Appending zero to any non-empty state makes product `0`, flips parity, and leaves sum unchanged. Starting `[0]` creates product `0`, odd length, sum `0`. `all0` and `all1` cache the OR of all even/odd bitsets, making zero processing O(1) big-int operations.
- **Sum bitsets:** `total = sum(nums)` bounds the absolute alternating sum. Bit index is `sum + total`; mask keeps bits in `[0, 2*total]`. Left shifts are masked; right shifts naturally drop sums below `-total`.
- **Answer selection:** Scan product categories `limit` down to `0`; the first category containing the target bit is the maximum valid product. `OVER` is ignored. If `limit` becomes reachable early, return it immediately.
- **Optimization:** `active` stores only reachable exact products `1..limit`. For positive `x`, exact products with `p*x > limit` are ORed into `high0/high1` and shifted once into `OVER`, reducing many shifts when `x` is large.
- **Complexity:** Let `R <= limit` be reachable exact products and `W = 2*total+1 <= 3601` bits. Time is roughly `O(n * (R + limit) * W / word_size)` big-int operations. Memory is `O(limit * W)` bits plus list overhead.
- **Edge cases:** Handles `limit=0`, all zeros, `abs(k) > total`, zeros after overflow, product `1` from ones, and does not count the empty subsequence for `k=0`.
- **Samples:** The three provided samples pass: `[1,2,3], k=2, limit=10 -> 6`; `[0,2,3], k=-5, limit=12 -> -1`; `[2,2,3,3], k=0, limit=9 -> 9`.
