Model the kept snowflake by its center vertex c. A neighbor v of c can serve as a branch vertex with y leaves iff it has at least y other neighbors in T, i.e. deg(v)-1 >= y; all other neighbors of v and of the chosen leaves can be deleted. For fixed c and y, the optimal choice is to keep every neighbor satisfying this condition, giving size 1 + (y+1)*count_c(y). Therefore, for each c, sort the capacities deg(v)-1 of its neighbors descending; if the i-th capacity is a_i, the best snowflake using i branch vertices has size 1 + i*(a_i+1) for a_i>0. Maximize this over all vertices and output N minus the maximum kept size.
