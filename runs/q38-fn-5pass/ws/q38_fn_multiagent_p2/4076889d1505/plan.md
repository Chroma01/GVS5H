Factor each N into prime powers. For each odd prime power p^e, use modulus p^{e+1} and residue 1+p, which has multiplicative order p^e; for 2^e, use modulus 4 with residue 3 when e=1, or modulus 2^{e+2} with residue 5 when e>=2, giving order 2^e. Combine all coprime component moduli and residues with CRT to obtain A and M. The order of A modulo M is the lcm of the component orders, hence exactly N. The product M is at most N^2 <= 10^18, and A can be kept in [1, M-1]. Handle N=1 separately by outputting 1 1.
