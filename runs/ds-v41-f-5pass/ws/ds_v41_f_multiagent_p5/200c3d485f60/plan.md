Model each adjacent pair as either equal or different; exactly k of the n-1 adjacent pairs must be equal. First choose which k positions among the n-1 gaps are equal, giving C(n-1, k) ways. Then choose arr[0] in m ways; for each of the remaining n-1-k gaps that are different, choose a value different from the previous element in m-1 ways. Thus the answer is C(n-1, k) * m * (m-1)^(n-1-k) modulo 1e9+7, with careful handling of m=1 and fast modular exponentiation/combinations.
