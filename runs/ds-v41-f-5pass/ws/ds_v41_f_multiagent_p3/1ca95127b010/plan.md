The operations preserve the relative order of 1s and move each 1 by a multiple of X, so the sequence of positions of 1s modulo X is invariant. Similarly, zeros move by multiples of Y, making the sequence of positions of 0s modulo Y invariant. These two invariants, together with equal counts of 1s, are necessary and sufficient. The solution is an O(N) linear scan comparing these residue sequences between S and T.
