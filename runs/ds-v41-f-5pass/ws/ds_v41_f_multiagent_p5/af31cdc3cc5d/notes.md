- **Problem shape:** Repeatedly delete adjacent pairs, score = absolute difference. Drawing arcs over original positions of each paired pair yields a non-crossing perfect matching (one leftover vertex when N is odd). Conversely every non-crossing matching (plus optional survivor) is realizable as a deletion order by peeling innermost arcs first.
- **Key formula:** For any set of 2m numbers, the max total of |diff| over a perfect matching is (sum of the m largest) − (sum of the m smallest). Reason: assign "+" to the m largest and "−" to the m smallest; value = sum(plus) − sum(minus) independent of which plus is matched to which minus, since every plus ≥ every minus. A non-crossing matching pairing each "+" position with a "−" position always exists for equal counts (repeatedly delete an adjacent opposite-colored pair; a two-color balanced sequence always has one unless empty).
- **Order independence:** Because the value of an even contiguous block depends only on its multiset, the arrangement inside the block is irrelevant. This is the crucial simplification.
- **Even N:** Answer = (sum of N/2 largest) − (sum of N/2 smallest). Sort and split.
- **Odd N:** The single survivor is unmatched. No arc may enclose an unmatched vertex (everything between a pair must vanish, but the survivor never does), so the survivor at position p splits the array into independent left [1,p−1] and right [p+1,N] blocks, both even ⇒ p is odd. Answer = max over odd p of prefVal(p−1) + sufVal(p+1), where prefVal/sufVal are the block values above.
- **Implementation:** Two-heap median structure (max-heap `lo` = smaller half, min-heap `hi` = larger half) maintains sumHi − sumLo, valid at even counts. Run left-to-right for prefix values and right-to-left (equivalently on the reversed array) for suffix values. Both passes are O(N log N); total O(N log N), O(N) memory.
- **Indexing details:** `suf_rev[L]` (L even) = value of the last L elements = suffix of length L starting at position n−L+1. For p odd, p+1 is even and the suffix length n−p is even; p=n gives length 0 = 0. p=1 always valid, so at least one candidate exists.
- **Edge cases:** all-equal ⇒ 0. N=2 ⇒ |a1−a2| (even case). Duplicates handled naturally by heap comparisons; boundary ties across the two halves give difference 0 and are safe. Sums up to ~3e14 fit Python int.
- **Validation:** Sample 1 (N=4, [1,2,5,3]) → 5; Sample 2 (N=7, [3,1,4,1,5,9,2]): p=1 gives 0+14 = 14, other p give 13/12/13 → 14; Sample 3 (all ones) → 0. Brute force over all deletion orders (memoized recursion on tuple states) agrees on these and on small random/hand cases (e.g. [1,10,1,10] → 18, [5,1,5,1] → 8).
- **Pitfalls avoided:** do not pick the survivor by value alone (position parity matters); keep heap sizes equal only at even counts; include empty prefix/suffix (value 0).
