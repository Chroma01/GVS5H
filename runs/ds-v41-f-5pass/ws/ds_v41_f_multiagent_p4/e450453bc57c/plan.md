Compute for every possible length-x window the minimum operations to make all its elements equal, then choose k non-overlapping windows minimizing total cost. The window cost is the sum of absolute deviations from the median, computable for all windows efficiently with a sliding median structure. Since k <= 15, a dynamic program over positions and selected-window count is feasible. Because all costs are nonnegative, requiring at least k windows is equivalent to requiring exactly k windows. Finally, validate with provided examples and brute-force small cases.
