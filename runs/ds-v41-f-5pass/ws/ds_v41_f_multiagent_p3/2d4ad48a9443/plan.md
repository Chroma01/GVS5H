The minimal increments to make a subarray non-decreasing equal the sum of `(prefix_max - a[i])` over the subarray. By reversing the array, this becomes a sum of suffix maximums, which can be maintained in O(1) amortized with a monotonic deque. Use two pointers over the reversed array: for each right endpoint, extend the window, and while the cost exceeds `k`, shrink from the left. The cost is `sum_of_suffix_max - sum_of_elements`. Count all valid subarrays ending at each right endpoint. Total time O(n), space O(n).
