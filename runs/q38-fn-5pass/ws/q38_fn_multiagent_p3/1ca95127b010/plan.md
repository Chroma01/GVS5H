The operations are inverse rewrites of the block pattern `0^X 1^Y <-> 1^Y 0^X`. In one move, the `Y` ones shift left by `X` positions and the `X` zeros shift right by `Y` positions, so the left-to-right order of all ones and all zeros is preserved. Consequently, each `1` keeps its position modulo `X`, and each `0` keeps its position modulo `Y`; these two ordered residue sequences are complete invariants for reachability. Thus the problem reduces to checking whether `S` and `T` have identical ordered lists of `1`-positions modulo `X` and identical ordered lists of `0`-positions modulo `Y`. This can be done in linear time by scanning the strings, without simulating any operations.
