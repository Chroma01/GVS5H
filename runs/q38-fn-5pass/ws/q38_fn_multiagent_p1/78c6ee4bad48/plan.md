The operation on four consecutive sorted pieces preserves the first coordinate and transforms the three gaps between them by swapping the first and third gaps while leaving the middle gap unchanged. Therefore each gap can only move within indices of the same parity, and adjacent swaps in each parity subsequence allow arbitrary permutations of odd-indexed gaps and even-indexed gaps independently. The total coordinate sum is N*x_1 plus a weighted sum of gaps with weight N-j for gap j; to minimize it, assign larger gaps to smaller weights within each parity, i.e. sort gaps of each parity ascending by position. Then compute the resulting minimum sum directly.
