- **Core reduction:** For a fixed center `c`, each neighbor `v` can serve as a middle vertex only with `y <= deg(v) - 1`. Let these capacities be `a_v`. If `x = k` middle vertices are chosen, the largest possible `y` is the `k`-th largest capacity among neighbors of `c`. Thus the best kept size centered at `c` is `max_k 1 + k * (a_k + 1)` over `k >= 1` with `a_k > 0`.
- **Fixed-center formula:** Sort positive capacities descending. For each prefix length `k`, using the top `k` neighbors as middles and `y = a_k` leaves each gives a valid snowflake of size `1 + k * (a_k + 1)`. The global answer is `N - max_c best_c`.
- **Why the construction is valid:** In a tree, for distinct neighbors `v` and `w` of the same center `c`, the sets `N(v) \ {c}` and `N(w) \ {c}` are disjoint; otherwise a cycle appears. Therefore leaves chosen for different middle vertices never collide, and deleting all other vertices leaves exactly the intended snowflake. Conversely, any snowflake centered at `c` must satisfy the same capacity bound.
- **Implementation details:** Read with `sys.stdin.buffer.readline` and manual `int()` conversion. Build adjacency lists and original degrees. Convert `deg` to capacities by subtracting one. Use `len(adj[c])` for the original center degree. Degree 1 and degree 2 centers are handled without sorting. For degree at least 3, collect only positive capacities, sort descending, and scan all prefixes. A one-positive-capacity case is also handled without sorting.
- **Complexity:** Sum of degrees is `O(N)`. Sorting cost is `sum d_c log d_c`, bounded by `O(N log N)`. Total collected capacities and scanned prefixes are `O(N)`. Memory is `O(N)`.
- **Edge cases:** `x` and `y` are positive, so zero capacities cannot be used. The center of the final snowflake may be a leaf in the original tree, which is essential for stars and paths of length 2. A degree-2 center whose two neighbors are leaves cannot form a snowflake centered there, but its leaf neighbors can. For `N >= 3`, a valid snowflake of size at least 3 always exists.
- **Performance choices:** Avoid sorting for degree 1/2 and for one positive capacity. Filter zero capacities before sorting to avoid useless work on stars. Use local variables for `adj` and `cap`. Early break if `best == n`. The direct center-by-center approach is sufficient; no DP or rerooting is needed.
- **Current status:** The optimized direct solution is implemented and should produce the minimum deletions as `N - best`.
