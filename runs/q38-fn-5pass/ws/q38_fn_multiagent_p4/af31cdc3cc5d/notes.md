- **Core characterization:** For any even-length segment of length 2m, the maximum total score is the sum of its largest m elements minus the sum of its smallest m elements. Upper bound: each removed pair contributes larger minus smaller, so the total is the sum of m chosen larger elements minus m chosen smaller elements, maximized by taking the top and bottom halves. Achievability: color the largest m elements positive and the smallest m negative. Since counts are equal, while both colors remain there is an adjacent opposite-color pair; deleting such pairs is valid and each score equals positive minus negative.
- **Odd N handling:** The final remaining element separates the original sequence into independent left and right parts. Both parts must have even length, so for 1-indexed positions the survivor must be odd, equivalently even in 0-based indexing. For a fixed survivor, the best total is the optimal score of the left even prefix plus the optimal score of the right even suffix.
- **Prefix/suffix computation:** Compute optimal scores for every even prefix and every even suffix. Maintain two heaps while scanning: a max-heap `low` storing negatives for the smallest ceil(length/2) values, and a min-heap `high` for the largest floor(length/2) values. Keep their sums. After each insertion, rebalance sizes so `len(low)` is either equal to `len(high)` or one larger. When the processed length is even, the two heaps have equal size and the segment score is `sum_high - sum_low`.
- **Combination:** If N is even, answer is `pref[N]`. If N is odd, compute suffix scores and maximize `pref[k] + suff[k + 1]` over even 0-based survivor positions `k`. `pref[0]` and `suff[N]` are zero.
- **Complexity:** Two heap scans give O(N log N) time and O(N) memory, suitable for N up to 3e5 in Python.
- **Implementation pitfalls:** Use correct parity: 0-based even survivor positions for odd N. The heap invariant must keep every value in `low` <= every value in `high`; insert into `low` when `x <= -low[0]`, otherwise into `high`, then rebalance. Duplicates at the median may be split arbitrarily and do not break correctness. Only even lengths have meaningful stored scores; odd entries can remain zero because they are not used.
- **Proof sanity checks:** N=2 matches absolute difference. N=3 reduces to choosing the better of the two adjacent pairs. Sample 2 is achieved by leaving the first element and optimally removing the remaining length-6 suffix.
- **Alternatives considered:** Fenwick/order-statistic structures over compressed values could also maintain top-half sums, but two heaps are simpler because only insertions are needed. Interval DP is too slow, and naive greedy adjacent removal is not reliable.
