Recognize that a 400 number must be of the form p^{2a}q^{2b} for two distinct primes p,q and positive exponents, equivalently N=M^2 where M has exactly two distinct prime factors. Since A≤10^12, M≤10^6, so we can precompute all valid M up to 10^6 and sort their squares. For each query, use binary search to find the largest precomputed square ≤ A. This gives efficient preprocessing and O(Q log V) query time. Careful attention is needed for the lower bound and exact exponent semantics.
