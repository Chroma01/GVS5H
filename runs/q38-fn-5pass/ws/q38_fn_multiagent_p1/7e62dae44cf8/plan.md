Model Takahashi's occupied cells as a growing connected region. The only relevant frontier information is the minimum-strength slime adjacent to the region, because absorbing any eligible slime only increases Takahashi's strength and never makes an already eligible slime ineligible. Use a min-heap of frontier cells keyed by slime strength, and repeatedly absorb the smallest frontier slime if its strength times X is strictly less than Takahashi's current strength. After absorbing it, add its strength and push its newly adjacent unabsorbed cells into the heap. If the smallest frontier slime is not absorbable, then no frontier slime is absorbable, so the process stops and the current strength is optimal.
