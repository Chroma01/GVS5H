Treat Takahashi as an expanding connected region: absorbed cells join the region and expose their unvisited orthogonal neighbors. Since Takahashi's strength only increases, the key decision is order; always considering the weakest currently adjacent slime is optimal. Maintain the frontier in a min-heap keyed by slime strength. If the smallest frontier slime satisfies `X * strength < current_sum`, absorb it and push its newly exposed neighbors. If the smallest cannot be absorbed, no larger frontier slime can be absorbed either, so the process stops. The answer is the final accumulated strength.
