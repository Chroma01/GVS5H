Reduce the deletion process to a noncrossing matching problem: each operation pairs two positions, and the total score is a signed sum where one element in each pair is added and the other subtracted. For any even-length segment, the optimum is obtained by taking its larger half as positive and its smaller half as negative. For odd N, the one unmatched element cannot lie inside any pair, so it must split the sequence into two independently matched even segments. Compute the best score for every even prefix and even suffix using the sum of their smallest halves, then combine over all valid split positions in O(N log N).
