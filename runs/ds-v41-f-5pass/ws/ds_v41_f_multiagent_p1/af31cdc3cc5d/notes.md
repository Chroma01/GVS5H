- **Model:** Each operation pairs two positions; the whole process is a noncrossing perfect matching (even N) or a noncrossing matching leaving one position unmatched (odd N). Score = sum over pairs of |A_i - A_j|.
- **Reachability:** Any noncrossing matching is achievable by removing innermost/adjacent pairs first. Conversely any run of operations yields a noncrossing matching.
- **Even segment value f(seg):** For a fully matched segment, |x-y| splits into one + and one - element. Any assignment of N/2 pluses and N/2 minuses is realizable: while both signs remain there is an adjacent opposite-sign pair (a sign change must occur), remove it, induct. So f(seg) = sum of larger half minus sum of smaller half, order-independent.
- **Even N answer:** f(whole array) directly.
- **Odd N structure:** The unmatched position p cannot be spanned by any pair, since everything between a pair must vanish for them to become adjacent, but p never vanishes. So p splits the array into two even fully-matched segments, and p must be odd (1-indexed). Answer = max over odd p of f(A[1..p-1]) + f(A[p+1..n]). Do NOT take a global sorted half-difference for odd N; counterexample [1,2,100] gives 99 globally but correct is 98 (match 2-100, leave 1).
- **Computing all f:** Maintain two heaps: max-heap `lower` (negatives) = smaller half, min-heap `upper` = larger half. Insert x into upper; move its min to lower; if lower larger than upper move its max back. Keep sizes lower=floor(L/2), upper=ceil(L/2) so at even L they are equal. Track sum_low/sum_up; record sum_up - sum_low only at even L.
- **Pref/suff combination:** left[L]=f(prefix L); reverse the array and compute pref[k]=f(last k elements) to get suffix values. For split p, right length = n-p, so use pref[n-p]. O(N log N) total.
- **Verified:** sample 1 (even, 5), sample 2 (odd, 14: p=1 → 0 + f([1,4,1,5,9,2])=14), sample 3 (0), and [1,2,100] → 98.
