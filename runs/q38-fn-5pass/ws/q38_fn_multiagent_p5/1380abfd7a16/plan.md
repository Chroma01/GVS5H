Process the insertions in reverse order to determine the final position of each number. When inserting `i` at position `P_i`, later insertions may shift it, but in the final array, `i` must occupy the `P_i`-th currently empty slot if we fill positions from `N` down to `1`. Use a Fenwick tree or segment tree over `N` empty slots to support finding the `k`-th empty position and marking it as filled. This gives an efficient `O(N log N)` solution suitable for `N <= 5 * 10^5`.
