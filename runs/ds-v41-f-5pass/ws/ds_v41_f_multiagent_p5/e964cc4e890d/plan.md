The key is to characterize strong connectivity via cuts of the path: for every cut between k and k+1, the matching must contain at least one edge from a white vertex after the cut to a black vertex before the cut, otherwise vertices on the right cannot return left. This turns the problem into counting W-B perfect matchings with a prefix constraint. A left-to-right scan can track unmatched black vertices, with unmatched white count determined by the prefix balance. The main challenge is optimizing the resulting DP for N up to 2e5 modulo 998244353.
