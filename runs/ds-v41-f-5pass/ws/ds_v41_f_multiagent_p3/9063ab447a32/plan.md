Use binary search on the total number of units. For a fixed K, compute the minimum cost to buy exactly K units by considering the marginal cost of the j-th unit of product i, which is P_i(2j-1). Select the K cheapest marginal costs across all products and sum them; K is feasible if this sum is at most M. Binary search the largest feasible K and output it. The main challenge is efficiently selecting/counting the K cheapest marginal costs in O(N log V) per check.
