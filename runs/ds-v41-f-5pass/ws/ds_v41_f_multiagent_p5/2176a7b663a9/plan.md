Key observation: G is the complement of an interval graph, so every connected pair has a path of length at most 3. If the intervals of s and t are disjoint, the answer is just W_s + W_t. Otherwise, the optimal path either uses one intermediate interval disjoint from both, or two intermediates placed on opposite sides of s and t. Precompute prefix/suffix minima over coordinates to get the cheapest interval with R < x or L > x in O(1). Each query then reduces to checking four cases and taking the minimum.
