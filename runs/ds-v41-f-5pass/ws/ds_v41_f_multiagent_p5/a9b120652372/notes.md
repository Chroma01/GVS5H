- **Model:** positions a1<...<am from A, b1<...<bk from B (0-indexed via bytes works since only differences matter). Order is preserved and pieces only merge, so require m>=k and (a_last-a_first)>=(b_last-b_first); otherwise -1.
- **Op effects on gaps:** a centre strictly inside the span reduces one gap by 2; a centre at a piece reduces the gap to the left and/or right by 1 each; a centre outside the span is a pure translation (reduces nothing). Span never increases.
- **Final structure:** exactly k-1 nonzero final gaps equal h_1..h_{k-1}, realised as an in-order subsequence of A's gaps g with g_i>=h_j; every other gap is driven to 0. D=(a_last-a_first)-(b_last-b_first) is the total span reduction and also equals sum of gap reductions regardless of partition.
- **Gap-reduction algebra (verified):** writing each reduction r_t=2x_t+y_t+y_{t+1} with y_s>=0 the count of centre-at-piece ops (y_1,y_m are endpoint/translation-coupled ops), feasibility needs y_t+y_{t+1}<=r_t and y_t+y_{t+1}==r_t (mod 2). Minimum ops = (D + y_1 + y_m)/2 + (#translation ops). The min over all free choices collapses exactly to the closed form below, confirming cost = max(u1+d1, ((u1+D)&1)+D-d1) for u1 in {0,1}.
- **Parity link:** the only binding constraint at a tight match (g_i=h_j) is Pg(i)^Ph(j)==u1, where Pg(i)=parity of gaps before i, Ph(j)=parity of h_1..h_{j-1}, and u1 in {0,1} is the free boundary parity (ops centred at the first piece). Loose matches (g_i>h_j) impose nothing. Independent of which gaps are skipped.
- **Greedy (O(N)):** for each u1 in {0,1}, scan gaps left to right, matching the current h_j to the earliest gap with g_i>=h_j and (g_i>h_j or Pg(i)^Ph(j)==u1). Earliest-feasible is optimal: gaps are consumed in increasing index order, so choosing the smallest index for h_j yields the largest candidate set for h_{j+1}. Feasible iff all k-1 h's get matched.
- **Cost:** for a fixed feasible u1 the minimum is max(u1+d1, ((u1+D)&1)+D-d1) with d1=b_first-a_first. Increasing u1 by 2 raises the first term and leaves the second unchanged, so only u1 in {0,1} matter; answer = min over feasible u1, else -1.
