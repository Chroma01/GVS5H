Operations A and B both swap an adjacent block of X zeros with a block of Y ones. Thus every 0 moves by exactly ±Y positions, so the ordered list of 0-positions modulo Y is invariant; every 1 moves by exactly ±X, so the ordered list of 1-positions modulo X is invariant. The total counts of 0s and 1s must also match. These invariants are sufficient to characterize reachability. The solution is an O(N) check of counts and these two ordered residue sequences.
