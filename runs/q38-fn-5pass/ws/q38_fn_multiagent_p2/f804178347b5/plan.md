Model the repeated majority operation as a complete ternary tree whose leaves are the characters of A. For each node, compute two costs: the minimum flips in its subtree needed to make the node output 0, and the minimum flips needed to make it output 1. Leaves have cost 0 for their current bit and cost 1 for the opposite bit. For an internal node and a target bit, combine its three children by choosing child outputs so that at least two children equal the target, taking the minimum additive cost. Process the tree bottom-up level by level until the root, then the answer is the smaller of the two root costs, since one corresponds to the unchanged final value and the other to changing it.
