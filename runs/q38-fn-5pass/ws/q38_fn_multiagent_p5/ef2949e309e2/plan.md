Use the middle index of the subsequence as the pivot. For each possible pivot index `k`, maintain frequency maps for values before `k` and after `k`, then count ways to choose two elements from the left and two from the right. Classify valid subsequences by how many additional copies of `nums[k]` are selected: if at least two extra copies are selected, the middle value is automatically the unique mode; if exactly one extra copy is selected, the three non-middle values must be pairwise distinct; if zero extra copies are selected, it is invalid. Compute these counts from the frequency maps in `O(distinct)` time per pivot, giving `O(n^2)` overall for `n <= 1000`, and accumulate the result modulo `10^9 + 7`.
