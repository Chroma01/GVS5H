A remaining Snowflake Tree is fully determined by its center vertex, the chosen middle neighbors of that center, and a common leaf count y. For a fixed center c, a neighbor v can be used as a middle vertex exactly when it has at least y neighbors other than c, i.e. deg(v)-1 >= y. Therefore, for each center, sort the values deg(v)-1 over its neighbors descending; if the k-th largest value is y, then keeping k middle vertices gives size 1 + k*(y+1). Maximizing this over all centers gives the largest possible remaining Snowflake Tree, and the answer is N minus that maximum.
