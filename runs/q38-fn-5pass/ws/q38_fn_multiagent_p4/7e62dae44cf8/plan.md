Model the cells already taken by Takahashi as one connected region containing the starting cell, with his current strength equal to the sum of the strengths in that region. A boundary cell can be absorbed exactly when its strength times X is strictly less than the current total strength, and absorbing any such cell only increases the total strength and the boundary, so it is always safe to absorb the weakest currently absorbable boundary cell. Maintain all boundary cells in a min-heap ordered by strength. Repeatedly pop the weakest boundary cell while it satisfies the strict condition, add its strength to the total, mark it absorbed, and push its unabsorbed neighbors. If the weakest boundary cell cannot be absorbed, no other boundary cell can be absorbed, so the current total is optimal.
