The key is that for any even-length segment, the maximum score equals the sum of its largest half minus the sum of its smallest half: this is an upper bound from the larger/smaller element of each pair, and it is achievable by coloring the largest half and smallest half and repeatedly pairing adjacent opposite colors. For odd N, the final remaining element separates the sequence into independent even-length left and right segments, so we only need to try every possible final position with the correct parity. Compute the optimal even-segment score for every prefix and suffix in O(N log N) using two heaps that maintain the smallest half and largest half. Then combine prefix and suffix scores to obtain the final answer.
