Use prefix sums to rewrite each subarray sum as the difference of two prefix sums. For each right endpoint, expand the K-th power via the binomial theorem and separate terms depending on the current prefix sum from accumulated powers of previous prefix sums. Maintain running sums of powers of prefix sums up to degree K, allowing the total to be computed in O(NK) or O(NK^2) time. Since K ≤ 10, this is easily fast enough, and all arithmetic is performed modulo 998244353.
