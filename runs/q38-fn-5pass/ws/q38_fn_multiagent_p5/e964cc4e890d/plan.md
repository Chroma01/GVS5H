Reduce strong connectivity to the condition that every prefix cut is crossed by at least one added edge from a white vertex on the right to a black vertex on the left. For a cut with `b` black vertices and `w` white vertices on the left, the cut is uncovered iff the first `b` black vertices are matched only to the first `w` white vertices; only the smallest `b` for each `w` with `w >= b` is relevant. Use inclusion-exclusion / first-bad-cut DP: if active constraints are `(b_i, w_i)`, compute `C_i = P(w_i,b_i) - sum_{j<i} C_j * P(w_i-b_j, b_i-b_j)`, then answer `N! - sum_i C_i * (N-b_i)!`. The remaining sums are of the form `sum C_j * (w_i-b_j)!`, which can be computed offline by CDQ divide-and-conquer with NTT using a shifted factorial convolution kernel. Handle `S[0]=='W'` or `S[-1]=='B'` as immediate zero, and if there are no active constraints the answer is `N!`.
