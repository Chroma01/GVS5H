- **Core equivalence:** A pairing can be charged to an attribute attaining each pair max, producing 2K selected cakes colored X/Y/Z with even color counts. Conversely, any even-colored selection can be paired inside each color, and actual pair prices are at least the charged sum. Thus the problem is exactly maximum-weight colored selection of exactly 2K cakes with parity mask 0.
- **Top-C upper bound:** Let M_i=max(X_i,Y_i,Z_i) and take C=2K cakes with largest M. Base value S=sum M over these cakes. Any feasible colored selection has value at most S.
- **Base coloring:** For each selected cake choose one max color as its base color. Let P be xor of base color bits. Since C is even, P is only 0,3,5,6. If P=0, answer is S.
- **Correction theorem:** Any feasible final selection differs from the base by recolors of kept selected cakes and swaps (drop selected i, add outside j with chosen color). Swap cost is M_i - value_j(color), nonnegative because selected M_i >= outside M_j. All useful operation toggles are in {3,5,6}. In this 2-dimensional GF(2) space, any multiset of toggles with xor P contains a submultiset of size at most 2 with xor P and no larger cost. Therefore the optimal loss from S is achieved by one operation of toggle P, or by two operations of the other two toggles.
- **Recolor candidates:** For each toggle, the three cheapest recolors (distinct selected items) are sufficient. In a two-operation correction, the other operation conflicts with at most one selected item, so one of three cheaper distinct recolors remains usable.
- **Swap candidates:** A swap of toggle t uses a selected item of one of two base colors and an outside item of the corresponding target color. For any fixed other operation, replacing a swap's selected item by one of the two smallest-M selected items of the same base color, and its outside item by one of the two largest-value outside items of the target color, preserves the toggle and does not increase cost while avoiding at most one selected/outside conflict. The code keeps top 5 for safety.
- **Enumeration:** Build candidate operations per toggle: top recolors plus generated swaps. Sort by cost. Check one operation for P, and pairs of operations for the other two toggles, rejecting conflicts on selected id and outside id.
- **Special cases:** K=1 is solved directly by top two values per attribute. C=N has no outside items, so only recolor candidates are needed. If base parity is already zero, output S immediately.
- **Complexity:** O(N log N) per test case due to sorting by M and a few small sorts; candidate enumeration is constant. This replaces the previous binary-search parity DP and is much faster on max-size inputs.
- **Implementation details:** Use global item ids to detect conflicts between selected and outside items. Swap costs are theoretically nonnegative; clamp tiny negatives to zero defensively. Operations are sorted so pair enumeration can break on cost.
